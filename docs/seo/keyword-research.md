# ShopiBuild: keyword research and content plan (UAE and Gulf)

Date: 3 November 2026. Markets: the UAE (main), then Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman. Languages: Arabic (default, `/`) and English (`/en`).

## How this was built

Without a paid tool (Semrush or Ahrefs), there are no exact monthly search volumes. Instead, each keyword was checked against what Google shows today:

1. **Live results** for each main term in Arabic and English: who ranks, and what kind of pages (agency, freelancer listing, guide, forum).
2. **Search intent**: buying (looking for a provider), commercial investigation (comparing), or informational (learning).
3. **Gaps**: where the results are weak, foreign or out of date, which is easier to win.

Priority = commercial value for ShopiBuild × how winnable the results are. When Search Console data arrives (2–4 weeks after the sitemap is submitted), re-rank this list on real impressions.

| شوبيفاي ام سلة ام زد | Saudi comparisons and old videos; nothing from a UAE angle | **High.** Comparison written for UAE and GCC sellers |

**Conclusion:** the Arabic results are the weak side. ShopiBuild can lead in Arabic by publishing guides that are accurate, specific to the UAE and priced in AED, then link them to the matching English pages with hreflang.

## Writing rules for every article

- One main keyword per article, in the title, the first paragraph, one H2 and the URL slug.
- Arabic is written for Gulf readers in clear Modern Standard Arabic, not translated from English. The English version is written separately for the same intent.
- A direct answer in the first 2–3 sentences (for featured snippets and AI answers), then the detail.
- An FAQ section at the end with 3–5 questions; it generates FAQPage structured data automatically.
- Prices in AED. Figures that change (fees, licences, plans) say "at the time of writing" and point to the official source.
- 3–5 internal links: to the homepage (`#pricing` and `#lead`) and to related articles.
- No claims about the platforms or the government that can't be checked.
