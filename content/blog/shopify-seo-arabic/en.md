**Short answer:** SEO for an Arabic Shopify store rests on five things: **keywords in your Gulf customers' own words**, **a separate Arabic URL** with hreflang, **collection and product pages written in Arabic** rather than translated, **a fast mobile store**, and **content that answers customers' questions**. Competition for Arabic keywords in the Gulf is much lower than for English, and that's your opportunity.

## Why Arabic SEO is a big opportunity right now

Search Google for "تصميم متجر شوبيفاي في الإمارات" (Shopify store design in the UAE) and look at the results: mostly freelancer service listings, with few and weak Arabic articles. The same is true in many categories. Shoppers search in Arabic while stores write only in English. A store that publishes strong Arabic content can rank much faster than it could competing in English.

## 1. Research keywords in your customers' language

Gulf shoppers don't search using formal translations. For example:

- "عطور عود" and "دهن عود" (oud perfume, oud oil), not just "oriental perfume".
- "عبايات" (abayas), not "traditional womenswear".
- "توصيل نفس اليوم دبي" (same-day delivery Dubai).

**How to find them:**
- Type the start of a phrase into Google and read the **autocomplete** suggestions.
- Check the **"People also ask"** box in the results.
- Ask your customer service team which questions keep coming up on WhatsApp.
- Once you have traffic, open **Google Search Console** to see the queries you appear for.

## 2. URL structure and languages

- **A separate URL for Arabic** and one for English, linked with **hreflang** tags. Shopify adds these automatically when languages are set up correctly.
- **Translate URL handles** where it makes sense, or keep them short and clear.
- Don't mix Arabic and English on the same page.

More on building the Arabic version in our [Arabic Shopify store](/en/blog/arabic-shopify-store-rtl) guide.

## 3. Collection pages: your strongest SEO pages

Collection pages are usually what ranks for terms like "women's perfume Dubai". Improve them with:

- **An SEO title** with the main keyword and the city or country, under about 60 characters.
- **A meta description** that earns the click, under about 155 characters.
- **A text block** under the products (150–300 words) explaining the collection and answering common questions.
- Internal links to related collections.

## 4. Product pages

- **A clear product name** containing the term shoppers search for.
- **An original Arabic description**, not copied from the supplier. Duplicate content doesn't rank.
- **Arabic alt text** describing each image.
- **Customer reviews**, which add fresh content and can show stars in search results.

## 5. Speed and mobile experience

Google evaluates your store using the mobile version. Things that slow Shopify stores down:

- Lots of apps, each adding scripts.
- Large, uncompressed images.
- Too many or heavy fonts.
- An unoptimised video at the top of the page.

Cut apps and build core features into the theme.

## 6. Structured data (schema)

Structured data helps Google understand your pages and can earn richer results such as price, availability and stars:

- **Product** on product pages (price, availability, rating).
- **Organization** for your store details.
- **BreadcrumbList** for navigation paths.
- **FAQPage** for FAQ content.

Most modern Shopify themes add Product data, but check it with Google's **Rich Results Test**.

## 7. Content: a blog that brings in customers

A blog isn't decoration. Each article should target a question your customers search before buying:

- "How do I tell real oud oil from fake?"
- "Best summer perfumes for the UAE"
- "Abaya sizes: how to choose the right one"

Link each article to the right collection or product. That's exactly what stores leading their categories do.

## 8. Don't forget AI search

Shoppers now ask ChatGPT and Gemini before they search Google. The same SEO basics help there, plus a few extra steps covered in our guide to [GEO: getting your store into AI answers](/en/blog/geo-ai-search-ecommerce).

## Quick SEO checklist

- [ ] Separate Arabic and English URLs with hreflang
- [ ] An Arabic SEO title and description for every collection and product
- [ ] A text block on every important collection page
- [ ] Original product descriptions, not copied
- [ ] Image alt text
- [ ] Good mobile speed
- [ ] Product structured data
- [ ] Site verified in Google Search Console, with the sitemap submitted
- [ ] Articles that answer customer questions

## FAQ

### How long does SEO take to show results?
Usually 3–6 months for medium-competition keywords. Lower-competition Arabic keywords can start moving within weeks.

### Is Shopify good for SEO?
Yes. It gives you clean URLs, an automatic sitemap, hreflang for languages, and control over titles and descriptions. Content and theme speed make the difference.

### Should I write in Modern Standard Arabic or Gulf dialect?
Write in clear, simple Arabic, and use the words customers actually search for, even when product names are colloquial.
