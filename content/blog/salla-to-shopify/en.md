**Short answer:** moving from **Salla to Shopify** is worth it when you need **custom design**, **selling to several countries**, or **more accurate ad tracking**. The steps: **export products and customers** from Salla, import them into Shopify and review variants, **build the new design**, **redirect every old URL** to its new one, connect **payments and shipping**, then **switch the domain** at a quiet time. The old store keeps running until the new one is ready.

## When is moving from Salla worth it?

Salla is an excellent platform for the Saudi market. Moving to Shopify makes sense when:
- Your brand needs **a distinctive visual identity** that templates can't deliver.
- You sell, or plan to sell, **outside Saudi Arabia**, especially from the UAE.
- You spend seriously on **ads** and need server-side tracking.
- You need **global integrations** with many apps and systems.

If your store is Saudi, sells locally and works well, staying on Salla may be the right call. See [Shopify vs Salla](/en/blog/shopify-vs-salla).

## Step 1: audit

- **Export your URL list**: products, collections and pages.
- Identify **pages that get traffic** in Google Search Console.
- List **the Salla services you rely on**: payment methods, couriers, apps and loyalty programmes.

## Step 2: export and move the data

- **Products:** export them from Salla, convert them to Shopify's import format, and check variants (sizes and colours), images and Arabic and English descriptions.
- **Customers:** export and import their data into Shopify. Passwords don't transfer, so customers reactivate their accounts via a link.
- **Past orders:** for history and reporting, as needed.

> Export methods and file formats change as both platforms update. Check Salla's current export tools and Shopify's import tools.

## Step 3: Shopify replacements for Salla services

| On Salla | On Shopify |
|---|---|
| Built-in payment methods | A gateway for your country, plus Tabby, Tamara and Apple Pay |
| Connected couriers | Courier and fulfillment apps and integrations |
| Templates | A custom Arabic and English theme |
| Apps | Shopify apps, or features built into the theme |

See [Shopify payment gateways](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae) and [Shopify shipping](/en/blog/shopify-shipping-uae).

## Step 4: redirects (the most important step for SEO)

Salla's URL formats differ from Shopify's. Every important old URL needs a **301 redirect** to its new equivalent, uploaded to Shopify as a file. Without them, old URLs in Google return "page not found".

## Step 5: launch

1. Test orders with every payment method.
2. Point the domain to Shopify at a quiet time.
3. Submit the new sitemap in Search Console.
4. Watch 404 errors for two weeks and add any missing redirects.
5. Message your customers about the new store.

The general guide for any platform is in [migrating to Shopify](/en/blog/migrate-to-shopify).

## FAQ

### Can I move from Salla to Shopify myself?
For small stores it's possible, but redirects, design and payments need experience to avoid losing sales or rankings.

### Will my Salla store go offline during the move?
No. It keeps running until the new store is ready and tested.

### Will I lose customer reviews?
You can usually bring them over with a reviews app that supports importing, depending on the export format available.
