**Short answer:** a **fulfillment centre** stores your products in its warehouse. When an order arrives in your store, it picks, packs and ships it to the customer, and it handles returns. It suits you once **packing and shipping take more of your time than marketing**, or when you need **consistently fast delivery** in the UAE. You typically pay **storage fees**, a **pick-and-pack fee per order** and **shipping fees**.

## How fulfillment works

1. **You send your stock** to the warehouse, where it's received and logged.
2. **A customer orders** from your Shopify store.
3. **The order reaches them automatically** through a Shopify integration.
4. **They pick and pack it**, in your branded packaging if you want.
5. **They ship it** with a courier, and the tracking number goes back to Shopify.
6. **They receive returns**, inspect them and restock.

## When do you need fulfillment?

Signs it's time:
- You spend hours a day packing instead of marketing.
- Your home or office has turned into a warehouse.
- Orders slip during peaks such as Ramadan and White Friday.
- You want to offer same-day or next-day delivery consistently.
- You plan to sell across the GCC.

**When you don't need it:** very low volume, made-to-order products, or when you need to handle every item yourself.

## Cost components

| Item | Usually charged |
|---|---|
| Receiving | Per shipment, carton or unit arriving at the warehouse |
| Storage | Monthly, by space (shelf, pallet or cubic metre) |
| Pick and pack | Per order, sometimes per extra item |
| Packaging materials | By type, or use your own |
| Shipping | By weight and zone |
| Returns | Per returned order |
| Cash on delivery | A collection fee, if enabled |

> Ask for a detailed quote based on your monthly orders and product sizes, and compare the **total cost per order**, not just the storage price.

## How to choose a fulfillment centre

Ask:

1. **Do they have a ready Shopify integration?** Orders must flow automatically.
2. **What's the cut-off time?** If an order arrives before a set hour, does it ship the same day?
3. **Where is the warehouse?** Being close to Dubai speeds up delivery.
4. **Do they support custom packaging** and thank-you cards?
5. **How do they handle returns?**
6. **Do they offer climate-controlled storage** for perfume and cosmetics?
7. **Do they ship to other GCC countries?**
8. **How will you see stock levels?** A dashboard, or automatic updates in Shopify.

Examples in the UAE market include **iQ Fulfillment** and **Quiqup**, among others.

## Connecting fulfillment to Shopify

- **Order sync:** every paid order transfers automatically.
- **Inventory sync:** Shopify stock levels update from the warehouse, so you don't sell out-of-stock items.
- **Tracking:** the tracking number returns to Shopify and reaches the customer, ideally on WhatsApp.

## FAQ

### How much does fulfillment cost in the UAE?
It depends on order volume and product size. Get quotes and compare the total cost per order, including storage, pick-and-pack and shipping.

### Can a small store use fulfillment?
Some providers accept small stores, but it pays off more as volume grows.

### Does fulfillment speed up delivery?
Usually yes, because the warehouse is built for fast picking and connected to couriers, and some providers offer same-day delivery.
