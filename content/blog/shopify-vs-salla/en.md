**Short answer:** Salla is an excellent Saudi platform for a store that sells inside Saudi Arabia and wants to start quickly at low cost. **Shopify is stronger** if your business is in the UAE, you sell to several countries, or you need a fully custom design and global ad and shipping integrations. The decision depends more on **your market** and **your plans** than on price.

## At a glance

| Factor | Shopify | Salla |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | Global (Canada) | Saudi Arabia |
| Strongest market | Global; well suited to the UAE and GCC | Saudi Arabia |
| Design freedom | Very high, fully codeable themes | Good, within the platform's templates |
| Arabic | Needs proper RTL setup | Arabic-first |
| Cross-border selling | Strong (Markets, currencies, languages) | More limited |
| Apps and integrations | Thousands of global apps | A growing local app store |
| Local payments | Third-party gateways in the UAE | Built-in local payment options for Saudi Arabia |
| Pricing | USD plan + transaction fees | Local plans, with a limited free option |

## Design: where Shopify pulls ahead

On Shopify you can build a fully custom theme: every page, section and animation. That matters for brands that want to stand out rather than look like their competitors.

On Salla, design happens within the platform's templates and settings. That's great for a quick start, but many stores end up looking alike, and deep customisation is more limited.

**Bottom line:** if visual identity is part of your brand's value (perfume, fashion, premium products), Shopify gives you far more room.

## Arabic and RTL

Salla is Arabic-first, and that's a real advantage. Shopify supports Arabic, including an Arabic checkout, but RTL quality depends on the theme and how the store is built. A Shopify store built properly in Arabic matches or beats Salla; one that relies on a translation app shows its flaws. See our guide to the [Arabic Shopify store](/en/blog/arabic-shopify-store-rtl).

## Payments

- **Salla:** built-in local payment options designed for the Saudi market.
- **Shopify in the UAE:** Shopify Payments isn't available, so you connect a third-party gateway and pay a Shopify transaction fee based on your plan (about 2% on Basic). In return, you can connect Tabby, Tamara, Apple Pay and local card gateways.

Details in [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

## Shipping

Salla connects easily with many Saudi couriers. Shopify connects with UAE couriers and fulfillment centres through apps and integrations, with more flexibility if you ship internationally. See [Shopify shipping in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-shipping-uae).

## Selling across countries

This is where Shopify clearly wins. From one store you can:
- Show prices in each country's currency.
- Set different prices and shipping per market.
- Serve the store in Arabic and English, with separate URLs per language that Google understands.

If your plan is to start in the UAE and expand to Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Qatar, Shopify saves you a rebuild later.

## Ads and tracking

Shopify has official integrations with Meta, Google, TikTok and Snapchat and supports server-side events. That means more accurate ad tracking, which matters most once you start spending seriously.

## Cost

Salla has affordable local plans and a limited free option, which usually makes it cheaper to start. Shopify starts at about AED 107 a month on annual billing, plus transaction fees in the UAE. The full breakdown is in [Shopify store cost in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-store-cost-uae).

> Confirm current plans and prices on each platform's official site, as they change often.

## Choose Salla if…

- Your business is in Saudi Arabia and sells only in Saudi Arabia.
- You want the lowest possible starting cost to test an idea.
- You don't need custom design and a template is enough.

## Choose Shopify if…

- Your business is in the UAE, or you sell across several GCC countries.
- Your brand needs a distinctive identity and custom design.
- You plan to spend on ads and need precise tracking.
- You want Arabic and English stores of equal quality.

## Can I move from Salla to Shopify later?

Yes. You can migrate products, customers and orders, but the design is rebuilt and old URLs must be redirected to the new ones so you keep your Google rankings. If you're unsure, pick the platform that fits your plans two years from now, not next week.

## FAQ

### Does Shopify support Arabic?
Yes. You can add Arabic as a store language, and the checkout appears in Arabic. RTL quality depends on the theme and how it's built.

### Is Salla available to UAE businesses?
Salla focuses on the Saudi market. If your business is registered in the UAE, check directly with Salla whether it supports your country and the payment methods you need.

### Which is better for SEO, Shopify or Salla?
Both can rank well with strong content. Shopify gives more control over structured data, theme speed and separate language URLs, which helps when competing in both Arabic and English.

### Which one is cheaper?
Salla is usually cheaper at the start. Shopify becomes more economical as sales grow and you expand to more countries, because you don't need to rebuild the store.
