**Short answer:** a successful perfume store in the UAE sells **an experience**, because shoppers can't smell a fragrance online. Make up for that with **precise descriptions of ingredients and notes**, **testers** at a token price, **reviews** that describe longevity and projection, **luxury design** that reflects the brand, **distinctive packaging**, and **fast, safe delivery**. Prepare the store before **Ramadan and both Eids**, which are peak sales periods.

## Why perfume is a strong category in the UAE

- Perfume, oud and bakhoor are part of daily life and occasions.
- Order values are relatively high, which supports ad costs.
- Repeat purchases: customers who love your scent come back.
- It suits gifting all year round.

## The biggest challenge: "I can't smell it"

### 1. Describe the notes clearly
- **Top, heart and base notes.**
- **The fragrance family:** oud, floral, woody, fresh.
- **Longevity and projection** in plain words: "lasts more than a day on clothes".
- **Occasion and season:** evening, summer, winter.

### 2. "If you like… try…"
Relating your fragrance to scents customers know helps them picture it (without claiming to copy other brands).

### 3. Samples
A set of **small samples** at a token price, with **a matching credit** off the full bottle. This is one of the strongest conversion tactics in perfume.

### 4. Reviews
Ask customers to describe **longevity and impression**. See [product page design](/en/blog/product-page-design).

## Design: luxury sells

- **Large images** with professional lighting and backgrounds that reflect the brand.
- **White space** and elegant type in Arabic and English.
- **A brand story page:** where the ingredients come from, and who creates the blends.
- **A fragrance finder (quiz):** "Which scent suits you?" leads shoppers to specific products.

Luxury perfume brands often benefit from a **custom theme**. See [custom theme vs template](/en/blog/custom-theme-vs-template).

## Products and variants

- **Sizes** (such as 3 ml, 6 ml and 12 ml for oils; 50 and 100 ml for perfumes) as variants.
- **Sets and bundles:** perfume + oil + bakhoor at a better price.
- **Gift wrapping** with a card.

## Shipping and storage

- **Safe packaging** that prevents breakage and leaks.
- **Proper storage** away from heat, especially in summer. Ask your fulfillment partner about **climate-controlled storage**. See [ecommerce fulfillment](/en/blog/fulfillment-centers-uae).
- **International shipping:** some perfumes (especially alcohol-based) face air freight or customs restrictions depending on destination. Check with your courier. See [shipping to the GCC](/en/blog/shipping-to-gcc).

## Payments

Premium perfume is well suited to instalments. Enable **Tabby and Tamara** and show them on the product page. See [Tabby and Tamara on Shopify](/en/blog/tabby-tamara-shopify).

## Marketing

- **Video** on Snapchat and TikTok: trying the scent and reacting, plus packaging. See [Snapchat and TikTok ads](/en/blog/snapchat-tiktok-ads-gulf).
- **Seasons:** Ramadan, both Eids, Mother's Day and National Day. See [Gulf ecommerce seasons](/en/blog/ramadan-white-friday-sales).
- **Content:** "How to choose genuine oud oil", "How to make your perfume last longer".
- **WhatsApp** for pre-purchase advice.

## FAQ

### Do I need a special licence to sell perfume?
You need a trade licence with a suitable activity, and extra requirements may apply depending on the product and emirate. Check with the relevant authority.

### How do I convince someone to buy a perfume they haven't smelled?
Precise descriptions, token-priced samples, reviews about longevity, and a clear policy.

### Can I sell perfume to Saudi Arabia?
Yes, taking shipping and customs restrictions into account. See [selling to Saudi Arabia from the UAE](/en/blog/sell-to-saudi-from-uae).
