**Short answer:** **Zid** is a strong Saudi platform for stores selling inside Saudi Arabia that want an easy start and ready-made local tools. **Shopify** is the stronger choice if your business is in the **UAE**, you sell to several countries, or your brand needs **custom design** and global ad integrations. Choose based on **your main market** and your expansion plans.

## At a glance

| Factor | Shopify | Zid |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | Global | Saudi Arabia |
| Strongest market | Global; well suited to the UAE and GCC | Saudi Arabia |
| Design | Fully custom theme | Platform templates and settings |
| Arabic | Needs proper RTL build | Arabic-first |
| Selling across countries | Very strong | Available, but focused on Saudi Arabia |
| Ads and tracking | Official Meta, Google, TikTok and Snapchat integrations | Basic integrations |
| Ecosystem | Thousands of apps and developers worldwide | A local app store |

## Zid's strengths

- **Arabic-first**, with an Arabic dashboard.
- **Local Saudi tools**: ready-made Saudi payment, shipping and integrations.
- **Marketplace integrations** in the region, such as selling through Amazon and Trendyol.
- An easy start for founders without technical experience.

## Shopify's strengths

- **Full design freedom**: your store doesn't look like anyone else's.
- **Selling to several markets** from one store, with currencies, languages, and different prices and shipping per country.
- **More precise ad tracking**, with official integrations and server-side events. See [Meta Pixel and CAPI on Shopify](/en/blog/meta-pixel-capi-shopify).
- **A huge global ecosystem** of apps and developers.

## If your business is in the UAE

Zid focuses mainly on the Saudi market, where most of its stores are. If your licence and accounts are in the UAE, confirm directly with Zid that it supports your setup and the payment methods you need. Shopify fully supports UAE businesses, although Shopify Payments isn't available, so you connect an external gateway. Details in [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

## Cost

Zid offers local plans priced for an easy start. Shopify starts at about AED 107 a month on yearly billing, plus transaction fees in the UAE. See [Shopify pricing in AED](/en/blog/shopify-plans-aed).

> Prices and features change. Compare current plans on the official websites before deciding.

## Choose Zid if…

- Your business is in Saudi Arabia and sells inside Saudi Arabia.
- You want ready-made Saudi tools with minimal setup.
- Templates are enough and you don't need custom design.

## Choose Shopify if…

- Your business is in the UAE, or you sell across several GCC countries.
- Your brand depends on a distinctive visual identity.
- You plan to spend seriously on ads.
- You want Arabic and English stores of equal quality.

## FAQ

### Is Zid available in the UAE?
Zid is Saudi and focuses on the Saudi market. Some UAE stores use it, but they're few. Check with Zid directly for your licence and country.

### Which is easier for beginners?
Zid is often easier for beginners in Saudi Arabia because it's Arabic-first. Shopify is easy too, and much easier when a specialist team builds it for you.

### Can I move from Zid to Shopify?
Yes. You migrate products and customers, rebuild the design, and redirect old URLs to the new ones to keep your Google rankings.

### Which is better for advertising?
Shopify, thanks to its official ad platform integrations and support for server-side events.
