**Short answer:** **Shopify Plus** is for larger brands and starts from about **$2,300 a month** (around AED 8,450). You typically need it once sales exceed **several million dirhams a year**, or when you need **checkout customisation**, **multiple stores** for different countries or brands, **advanced automation** or **full B2B**. Most UAE stores are well served by Basic, Grow or Advanced for years.

## What sets Shopify Plus apart?

| Feature | Standard plans | Shopify Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Checkout customisation | Limited | Extensive (via Checkout Extensibility) |
| Multiple stores | One store per subscription | Several stores within the plan |
| Automation | Basic automation | Advanced Shopify Flow, scheduled launches and offers |
| B2B | Core features (up to 3 catalogs) | Full B2B with unlimited catalogs |
| Staff accounts | Limited by plan | Effectively unlimited |
| Support | Standard | Priority |
| Peak performance | Good | Built for very high traffic |

> Features and pricing change. Confirm current details directly with Shopify.

## When is upgrading worth it?

Signs you need Plus:

- **Sales** are large enough that transaction fees and the subscription have become a significant line item. Plus usually makes financial sense at annual sales in the millions.
- **Several markets or brands:** a UAE store and a Saudi store with different teams and pricing, or more than one brand.
- **Checkout:** you need customisations such as extra fields, in-checkout offers or custom payment logic.
- **Wholesale (B2B):** you need many catalogs and customer-specific pricing at scale. See [B2B wholesale on Shopify](/en/blog/b2b-wholesale-shopify).
- **Big launches:** product drops and seasonal sales with very high traffic.

## When you don't need it

- Your sales are well below that level.
- One store for one market.
- The standard checkout works for you.

In that case, **Advanced** or **Grow** is the smarter choice. See [Shopify pricing in AED](/en/blog/shopify-plans-aed).

## A note on the UAE

Even on Plus, **Shopify Payments isn't available in the UAE** at the time of writing, so you use external gateways. Ask Shopify about Plus transaction fees with external gateways, as they're part of the calculation. See [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

## Moving to Plus

- Moving from a standard plan to Plus is **an account upgrade**, not a store migration.
- Plan **checkout customisation** and **automation** from the start to get value from the investment.
- If you're coming from another platform, see [migrating to Shopify](/en/blog/migrate-to-shopify).

## FAQ

### How much does Shopify Plus cost?
It starts from about $2,300 a month, and may vary with sales volume and contract. Ask Shopify for the current offer.

### Does Plus solve the Shopify Payments issue in the UAE?
No. Shopify Payments depends on the merchant's country and isn't available in the UAE at the time of writing.

### Can I sell wholesale without Plus?
Yes. Core B2B features are now available on standard plans, with limits such as the number of catalogs.
