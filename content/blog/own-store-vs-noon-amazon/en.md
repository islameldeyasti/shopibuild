**Short answer:** **Noon and Amazon** give you **ready-made traffic** quickly, in exchange for **a commission on every sale**, direct price competition and a limited relationship with the customer. **Your own Shopify store** gives you **the brand, the data, the customer and higher margins**, but you have to bring the traffic yourself. The smartest strategy for most UAE brands is **both**: marketplaces for reach, your own store for the relationship and profit.

## At a glance

| | Noon and Amazon | Your own store |
|---|---|---|
| Traffic | Ready from day one | You bring it with ads and SEO |
| Fees | Commission per sale + other fees | Subscription + payment fees |
| Competition | Side by side with competitors | Your store alone |
| Brand | Limited by the platform's layout | Full design and identity |
| Customer data | Very limited | Complete: email, phone, purchase history |
| Remarketing | Difficult | WhatsApp, email, custom audiences |
| Rules | The platform sets and changes them | You set them |

> Noon and Amazon fees vary by category and fulfillment type, and change over time. Check each platform's official fee schedule.

## When to start with marketplaces

- Your product is **well known and searched by name** (electronics, consumer goods).
- You want to **test demand** without a big ad budget.
- You hold **stock** and can benefit from the platform's storage and delivery services.

## When to start with your own store

- Your product is **a brand** that depends on story and design (perfume, fashion, abayas, premium goods).
- Your **margins** can't carry commissions and price competition.
- You want to **build a customer base** that buys again.
- You want control over **experience, pricing and promotions**.

## The hybrid strategy

Many successful UAE brands work like this:

1. **Marketplaces** for some products, especially best-sellers, to reach new customers.
2. **Your own store** for the full range, exclusive releases and special offers.
3. **Packaging** that invites customers to your store: a card with a discount on their next order from your site.
4. **Consistent pricing** so you don't undercut your store or break marketplace policies.

That way, the marketplace brings the customer the first time, and your store keeps them.

## A simple calculation

If the total marketplace cost (commission plus other fees) is around 15%–25% of the sale price, and your own store costs about 5% in payment fees plus ad spend per order, the question is: **is your cost to acquire a customer lower than the marketplace's cut?** For repeat orders it usually is, because the second and third orders from your store carry almost no ad cost.

## Repeat customers: the real difference

A customer on a marketplace is "the marketplace's customer". A customer in your store is "your customer": you can message them on WhatsApp, show them new products and reward them. That's where your own store becomes more profitable over time.

## FAQ

### Can I sell on Noon, Amazon and my own store at the same time?
Yes, and many brands do. Sync inventory so you don't sell items that are out of stock.

### Which is more profitable?
Your own store is usually more profitable long-term thanks to repeat customers; marketplaces are faster at the start.

### Do I need a licence to sell on Noon and Amazon?
Yes. The platforms require a trade licence from UAE sellers. See [ecommerce licence in the UAE](/en/blog/ecommerce-license-uae).
