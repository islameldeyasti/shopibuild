**Short answer:** Shopify stores in the UAE ship in one of two ways: **pack orders yourself** and hand them to a local courier such as Aramex, Jeebly, Quiqup or Emirates Post, or use a **fulfillment centre** that stores, packs and ships your products automatically. Delivery within one or two days is now the norm in the UAE, and many Dubai shoppers expect same-day delivery.

## Option 1: Ship it yourself with a courier

You receive the order, pack it and print the waybill, and a driver collects it.

**Best for:** new stores, handmade products and low order volumes.

**Common couriers in the UAE:**

| Courier | Strong at |
|---|---|
| Aramex | Wide local, GCC and international coverage |
| Jeebly | Fast local delivery for online stores |
| Quiqup | Same-day delivery and fulfillment |
| Emirates Post | Coverage across all emirates at competitive prices |
| DHL, FedEx and UPS | Fast international shipping |

> Rates depend on weight, zone and monthly volume. Get quotes from several couriers and renegotiate as your volume grows.

## Option 2: A fulfillment centre

You send your stock to the provider's warehouse. As soon as an order lands in Shopify, their team picks, packs and ships it.

**Pros:**
- No warehouse or packing team of your own.
- Faster dispatch, with same-day delivery in many cities.
- Returns handled for you.

**Cost:** monthly storage fees, a pick-and-pack fee per order, and shipping fees.

**Best for:** stores shipping dozens of orders a day or more, or founders who want to focus on marketing instead of packing.

Examples in the market include **iQ Fulfillment** and **Quiqup**, among others.

## Connecting shipping to Shopify

The key is that **orders reach the courier automatically**, with no copying and pasting. Most providers offer a Shopify app or integration that:

1. Pulls the order as soon as payment is confirmed.
2. Prints the waybill.
3. Sends the **tracking number** back to Shopify and to the customer.
4. Updates the order status on delivery.

**Send tracking to customers on WhatsApp**, not just email. Gulf shoppers follow up on WhatsApp far more.

## Setting up shipping zones and rates in Shopify

Under **Settings → Shipping and delivery**, set:

- **A UAE zone:** a flat rate, or free shipping above a threshold (for example AED 200).
- **A GCC zone:** rates by country or weight.
- **Express delivery:** a paid same-day option in Dubai.
- **Local pickup**, if you have a collection point.

**Tip:** a free-shipping threshold raises average order value. Set it slightly above your current average basket.

## Shipping to other GCC countries

When you ship from the UAE to Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain or Oman:

- Plan for customs and import duties in each country, and tell customers clearly who pays them.
- Show prices in the local currency.
- State delivery times honestly; they are usually longer than domestic.

## Cash on delivery and shipping

COD lifts sales in some categories, but it also raises **refused deliveries**. Reduce them with:
- WhatsApp order confirmation before dispatch.
- A small COD fee.
- Disabling COD in areas or for products with high refusal rates.

Payment options are covered in [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

## Your return policy

Write a clear return policy in Arabic and English and show it on the product page. Cover the return window, the product condition required, and who pays return shipping. A clear policy builds trust and sales.

## FAQ

### Which is the best courier for a Shopify store in the UAE?
It depends on your volume and region. For fast local delivery try Jeebly or Quiqup, for wide UAE and GCC coverage Aramex, and for international DHL, FedEx or UPS. Compare rates and how well each integrates with Shopify.

### When should I switch to a fulfillment centre?
When packing and shipping take more of your time than marketing and sales, or when you need consistent same-day delivery.

### Can I offer same-day delivery in Dubai?
Yes, with local couriers or fulfillment centres that support it, as long as your stock is held inside the UAE.
