**Short answer:** **Shopify Payments is not available to UAE-registered merchants** at the time of writing. You need a **third-party gateway** for cards and Apple Pay, **Tabby and Tamara** for instalments, and **cash on delivery** if it suits your product. Shopify charges a transaction fee on orders paid through an external gateway, based on your plan (about 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow and 0.6% on Advanced).

## What UAE shoppers expect at checkout

UAE customers are used to fast, flexible ways to pay:

1. **Cards** (Visa and Mastercard).
2. **Apple Pay** and Google Pay, the fastest options on mobile.
3. **Buy now, pay later** with Tabby and Tamara.
4. **Cash on delivery** in some categories and for newer stores.

Every missing option means a share of shoppers abandon the cart.

## 1. A gateway for cards and Apple Pay

With Shopify Payments unavailable, you choose an external gateway that supports Shopify and operates in the UAE. Well-known names in the market include **Tap Payments**, **Telr**, **PayTabs** and **Network International**, among others.

**How to choose between them:**

| Factor | What to ask |
|---|---|
| Fees | What's the percentage per transaction? Is there a fixed fee? What about international cards? |
| Apple Pay | Is it supported directly inside Shopify checkout? |
| Payouts | How many days until funds arrive, and in which currency? |
| Licence | Do they accept your licence type (e.g. e-Trader)? |
| Checkout experience | Does payment happen inside checkout, or redirect to an external page? |
| Support | Is there local support in Arabic? |

> The current list of gateways available to your store is in Shopify admin under **Settings → Payments**. Confirm current fees with each provider directly.

**Tip:** a gateway that keeps the shopper inside Shopify's checkout gives a smoother experience and more trust than one that redirects them to another site.

## 2. Tabby and Tamara: instalments that lift order value

Buy-now-pay-later is very popular in the UAE and Saudi Arabia, especially in fashion, perfume and electronics. Shoppers split the amount into interest-free payments, and you're paid by the provider.

**How to connect them:**
1. Apply for a merchant account with Tabby, Tamara or both, and wait for approval.
2. Install each provider's official Shopify integration.
3. Show the instalment message on the **product page** itself (for example "split into 4 payments"), not just at checkout. That's what influences the buying decision.

> Tabby and Tamara fees and approval terms are set by each provider and are usually higher than card fees. Factor them into your margins.

## 3. Cash on delivery (COD)

Shopify lets you add cash on delivery as a manual payment method. It helps new stores that haven't built trust yet, but it has costs:

- Refused deliveries, where you pay shipping both ways.
- Collection fees from the courier.
- Slower access to your money.

**Tip:** enable it with WhatsApp order confirmation before shipping, and consider a small COD fee or a minimum order value.

## 4. The real cost per order

Example: a AED 300 order on the Basic plan through a gateway charging 2.75%:

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Gateway fee (2.75%) | AED 8.25 |
| Shopify transaction fee (2%) | AED 6.00 |
| **Total** | **AED 14.25 (4.75%)** |

On Grow, Shopify's fee drops to 1%, bringing the total to about 3.75%. Upgrading usually pays off once monthly sales pass roughly AED 18,000. Details in [Shopify store cost in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-store-cost-uae).

## FAQ

### Is Shopify Payments available in the UAE?
No, not at the time of writing. Shopify hasn't announced a date for the UAE, so you need a third-party gateway.

### Can I enable Apple Pay on a Shopify store in the UAE?
Yes, through a gateway that supports Apple Pay with Shopify. Confirm with the gateway that Apple Pay appears directly inside checkout.

### Can I use Tabby and Tamara with Shopify?
Yes. Both offer Shopify integrations, and you need an approved merchant account with each.

### Why does Shopify charge a transaction fee when I already pay a subscription?
Because you're using an external gateway instead of Shopify Payments. The rate depends on your plan and drops as you upgrade.
