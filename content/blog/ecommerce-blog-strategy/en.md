**Short answer:** a successful store blog answers **the questions customers search before buying** and links each article **to the right collection or product**. Start with **10–20 articles** targeting real keywords in Arabic and English, publish consistently, and update older posts. Results usually start after **3–6 months**, then bring steady free traffic.

## Why should a store have a blog?

- **Free traffic:** instead of paying for every visitor through ads.
- **Trust:** shoppers who learn from you buy from you.
- **AI search:** clear articles become sources for ChatGPT and Gemini answers. See [GEO](/en/blog/geo-ai-search-ecommerce).
- **Content for social:** every article feeds Instagram, WhatsApp and email.

## Article types that sell

### 1. Buying guides
"How to choose genuine oud oil", "How to choose your abaya size". The shopper is close to buying, and the article guides them to your products.

### 2. Comparisons
"Cambodian or Indian oud?" High buying intent.

### 3. "Best of" lists
"7 best summer perfumes for the UAE". These rank easily when useful and honest.

### 4. Usage and care
"How to make perfume last longer", "How to wash a crepe abaya". They reduce returns and build loyalty.

### 5. Seasons and occasions
"Ramadan gifts for the family". See [Gulf ecommerce seasons](/en/blog/ramadan-white-friday-sales).

## How to choose topics

1. Collect **customer questions** from WhatsApp and comments.
2. Search them on Google and note **suggestions** and **"People also ask"**.
3. Rank them by **how close they are to purchase**.
4. Give each main keyword **one article**.

Details in [Arabic keyword research](/en/blog/arabic-keyword-research).

## The structure of an article that ranks

- **A title** containing the main keyword.
- **A direct answer** in the first two paragraphs.
- Clear **subheadings**.
- **Tables or lists** for comparisons and steps.
- **Internal links** to products, collections and related articles.
- **FAQs** at the end.
- **Real photos** of your products.

## Linking to products: the part everyone forgets

An article without product links is a wasted visit. In every article:
- In-text links to the products mentioned.
- A "recommended products" section at the end.
- A link from the collection page to the related article, and back.

## Arabic and English

Write a version for each language on the same topic, linked with hreflang. Don't translate literally: each audience has its own words and questions. See [bilingual Shopify store](/en/blog/bilingual-shopify-store).

## Cadence

- To start: **a batch of 10** core articles.
- Then: **one or two a week**, consistently. That beats ten at once and then silence.
- **Update older articles** every 6–12 months (prices, dates, facts).

## Measuring results

- **Search Console:** impressions, clicks and queries per article.
- **Google Analytics:** blog traffic and the sales it contributes to.
- **Rankings:** articles in positions 8–20 are worth improving first.

## FAQ

### How many articles do I need to see results?
Start with 10–20 strong articles; results usually begin within 3–6 months.

### Can I use AI to write?
As a helper, yes, but content must be accurate, useful and grounded in your real expertise. Weak content doesn't rank.

### What's the ideal article length?
Whatever fully answers the question. Most good articles are 800–2,000 words.
