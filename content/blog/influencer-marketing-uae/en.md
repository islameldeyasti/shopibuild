**Short answer:** since **1 February 2026**, any promotional content targeting UAE audiences requires an **Advertiser Permit**, whether paid or in exchange for products. In practice: **work only with influencers who hold the permit**, and make sure your contract covers it. Then choose influencers by **real audience and credibility**, not follower count, and use **discount codes and tracking links** to measure actual sales.

> This article is general information. Regulations, the responsible authority and penalties may change. Confirm current requirements with the official UAE media regulator.

## The regulatory framework in brief

- **An Advertiser Permit** is now required for anyone publishing promotional content aimed at the UAE: influencers, creators and agencies.
- It covers **paid** and **unpaid** content (such as free products in exchange for posts).
- **Violations** carry fines; sources cite different amounts, so check the official text.

**As a brand:** ask influencers for their **permit number** before collaborating and keep it with the contract. Promotional content should also be **clearly labelled as advertising** for followers.

## How to choose an influencer

| Criterion | What to ask |
|---|---|
| Audience | Are their followers actually in the UAE or the GCC? What ages and interests? |
| Engagement | Are comments real and relevant to the content? |
| Credibility | Do they promote everything, or are they selective? |
| Fit | Does their content suit your brand and values? |
| Past results | Can they share results from previous campaigns? |
| Permit | Do they hold a valid Advertiser Permit? |

**Tip:** smaller (micro) influencers often have higher engagement, lower cost and a more precise audience, which suits new stores well.

## Types of collaboration

- **A paid post or story.**
- **Product in exchange for content** (with the permit).
- **Commission on sales** via a code or link.
- **Content for ads:** buy the right to use an influencer's video in your Snapchat, TikTok and Meta ads. See [Snapchat and TikTok ads](/en/blog/snapchat-tiktok-ads-gulf).
- **A long-term brand ambassador.**

## Measure sales, not likes

- **A unique discount code** per influencer (such as SARA10).
- **A UTM-tracked link** per influencer.
- In Shopify, measure **orders**, **order value** and **new customers** per code.
- Calculate **cost per order** for each influencer and compare it with your paid ads.

## Prepare your store before the campaign

An influencer can send a lot of traffic in a short time. Make sure:
- **The page is fast** and can handle the load. See [Shopify store speed](/en/blog/shopify-store-speed).
- **The product is in stock** in sufficient quantity.
- **The product page** is persuasive. See [product page design](/en/blog/product-page-design).
- **The code works** before the post goes live.

## FAQ

### Do I need a permit as a store advertising on my own accounts?
The regulations cover online promotional content broadly. Confirm your specific situation with the official authority.

### How much does an influencer collaboration cost?
It varies widely by audience size, platform and content type. Test small and measure before committing large amounts.

### Is one big influencer or several small ones better?
For new stores, several smaller influencers usually deliver better results for the cost.
