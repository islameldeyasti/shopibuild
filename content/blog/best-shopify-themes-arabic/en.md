**Short answer:** there's no single "best theme" for Arabic. The best theme for you **genuinely supports RTL** in every element (menus, sliders, filters, cart), is **fast on mobile** and suits **your product category**. Don't rely on the description: **test the demo in Arabic on your phone** before buying. Even popular free themes have disputed Arabic support, so check for yourself.

## Why choosing a theme for Arabic is hard

Most Shopify themes are designed for English first. Some support RTL partially: the text flips, but sliders, arrows and menus don't. Many store owners only discover this after buying the theme and building the store.

## Signs of real RTL support

Before buying, check:

1. **The description mentions RTL explicitly**, ideally Arabic by name.
2. **There's a demo in Arabic** or another RTL language you can try.
3. **The mobile menu** opens from the right.
4. **Sliders** move in the right direction, with mirrored arrows.
5. **Filters and sorting** display correctly.
6. **The cart drawer** opens from the correct side.
7. **Reviews** from Arab or Middle East merchants.

## A 10-minute test before buying

Open the demo on **your phone**, switch to Arabic if available, and try:

- [ ] Open the menu and browse collections
- [ ] Swipe the homepage slider
- [ ] Open a product page and change images and size
- [ ] Add to cart and open the cart
- [ ] Use filters on a collection page
- [ ] Check that a price like "120 درهم" displays correctly

If more than two of these fail, the theme will cost you a lot of fixes.

## What to choose for your category

- **Perfume and luxury:** large imagery, white space, elegant type, a product page that tells a story.
- **Fashion and abayas:** strong filters (size, colour, fabric), multiple photos per product, a size guide.
- **Electronics:** comparison, clear specs, fast search.
- **Large catalogues:** mega menus, excellent search and filters.

## When do you need a custom theme?

- When you can't find a theme that handles Arabic properly in every element.
- When you want a distinctive identity that doesn't look like your competitors.
- When you need specific features instead of a stack of apps.

## FAQ

### Does the free Dawn theme support Arabic?
It's widely debated. Some merchants report RTL problems with it, while some sources say it works with certain settings. Test it yourself in Arabic on mobile before relying on it.

### Do RTL apps fix the problem?
They fix part of it temporarily, but add slowdown and a monthly fee, and don't cover every element.

### How much does a paid Shopify theme cost?
Usually around $150–$400, one-off, with updates from the developer.
