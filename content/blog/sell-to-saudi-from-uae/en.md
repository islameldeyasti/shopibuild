**Short answer:** Saudi Arabia is **the Gulf's largest ecommerce market**, and you can sell to it from your UAE store. Watch for **Saudi VAT (15%)**, **customs duties** that low-value shipments are often exempt from (sources cite around **SAR 1,000**, with specific rules), **pricing in riyals**, **clarity on who pays duties**, and **honest delivery times**. As sales grow, consider **stock inside Saudi Arabia**.

> Customs and tax rules change and depend on product type, value and origin. Confirm with ZATCA (the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority) and your courier before relying on any figure.

## Why Saudi Arabia?

- A large market with high purchasing power.
- A close language and culture, with many products in demand in both countries.
- Widespread digital payments and instalments.

## Shipping

- Couriers with GCC coverage such as Aramex, DHL and others.
- A clear **commercial invoice** with every shipment.
- Delivery is usually longer than domestic, so state it clearly. See [shipping from the UAE to the GCC](/en/blog/shipping-to-gcc).

## Customs and tax

- **Saudi VAT is 15%**, usually calculated on the shipment value including shipping and duties.
- **Customs duties:** low-value shipments are often exempt from customs duty, with sources citing a threshold around **SAR 1,000**. How VAT applies to small parcels varies between sources.
- **Origin of goods:** GCC-origin products that meet origin rules may receive preferential treatment; check the conditions.
- **Restricted products:** some categories (such as certain cosmetics and supplements) need registration or approvals in Saudi Arabia.

**Most important:** decide **who pays duties** and say so before checkout. A "duties paid" option (DDP) gives a better experience and reduces refused deliveries.

## Pricing and currency in Shopify

- Create **a Saudi market** in Shopify.
- Show prices **in Saudi riyals**, with automatic conversion or fixed prices.
- Set **shipping rates** specific to Saudi Arabia.
- Build duties and VAT into the price or the shipping, depending on your strategy.

## Payments

- **Tabby and Tamara** operate in Saudi Arabia, and Tamara is especially strong there. See [Tabby and Tamara on Shopify](/en/blog/tabby-tamara-shopify).
- Check that your gateway accepts **Saudi cards** (including mada, if your gateway supports it) and at what fee. See [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

## Marketing to Saudi Arabia

- **Snapchat** is very strong in Saudi Arabia. See [Snapchat and TikTok ads](/en/blog/snapchat-tiktok-ads-gulf).
- **Saudi-dialect content** in targeted ads.
- **Saudi seasons:** Ramadan, both Eids, Saudi National Day (23 September) and Founding Day (22 February).

## When to open stock or an entity in Saudi Arabia

- When **Saudi Arabia becomes a large share** of your sales.
- When **delivery times and duties** are holding back growth.
- **Stock at a Saudi fulfillment centre** cuts delivery to a few days and reduces customs per order.
- **A Saudi entity** has its own registration and tax requirements, so get specialist advice.

## FAQ

### Can I sell to Saudi Arabia without a Saudi company?
Yes, as a cross-border sale from the UAE, taking customs, tax and product-category requirements into account.

### Who pays Saudi import VAT?
You decide: include it in the price or shipping, or the customer pays on delivery. Make it clear before checkout.

### How long does shipping to Saudi Arabia take?
It depends on the city and courier. Ask for actual times to Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam.
