**Short answer:** most visitors who add to cart don't complete the purchase. You can recover some of them with **a short message sequence**: the first after **about an hour** (a simple reminder), the second after **a day** (answering a question or doubt), and the third after **two to three days** (an incentive if needed). In the Gulf, **WhatsApp** usually beats email, provided you have **customer consent**. But first, fix the reasons carts are abandoned.

## Why do shoppers abandon carts?

- **Surprise shipping costs** or VAT appearing at checkout.
- **Missing payment options**: no Apple Pay or instalments.
- **Unclear** or long delivery times.
- **Forced account creation**.
- **A slow** or confusing mobile checkout.
- **Just browsing** and comparing.

Fix the first causes before relying on messages. See [increase your conversion rate](/en/blog/increase-conversion-rate).

## Setting it up in Shopify

- In **Settings → Notifications**, enable the **abandoned checkout** email.
- Or use Shopify's **marketing automations** for a message sequence.
- For WhatsApp, you need the **WhatsApp Business API** through an approved provider and an app connecting it to Shopify. See [WhatsApp marketing](/en/blog/whatsapp-marketing-shopify).

**Important:** recovery messages go to shoppers who entered their email or number at checkout, and **marketing messages need consent**.

## A suggested message sequence

| Message | Timing | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | About 1 hour later | A friendly reminder with the product image and cart link |
| 2 | After 24 hours | Information that removes doubt: delivery, returns, instalments or reviews |
| 3 | After 48–72 hours | A small incentive (free shipping or a gift) if needed |

## Message examples

**Message 1 (WhatsApp):**
> "Hi Noura 👋 you left the black crepe abaya in your cart. It's still reserved for you; complete your order here: [link]. Delivered within a day in Dubai."

**Message 2:**
> "Any questions about sizing? Reply to this message and we'll help. You can also split the amount into 4 payments with Tabby."

**Message 3:**
> "Free shipping on your order if you complete it within 24 hours: [link]."

## Tips

- **Don't lead with a discount.** Many shoppers return after a reminder alone, and instant discounts teach customers to abandon carts.
- **Short, personal messages**, in Arabic or the shopper's language.
- **The product image** in the message.
- **A direct link** that restores the same cart.
- **A real WhatsApp number** that a person answers.
- **Stop the sequence** as soon as the shopper buys.

## Measure results

- The share of carts recovered.
- Sales from each message.
- The best channel: WhatsApp or email.
- The incentive's impact: is it worth the cost?

## FAQ

### Does Shopify send abandoned cart messages automatically?
Yes. The abandoned checkout email is built into Shopify, and you can extend it with marketing automations.

### Can I message on WhatsApp without consent?
Not for marketing messages. Collect consent at checkout.

### Should the first message include a discount?
Preferably not. Start with a reminder, and use an incentive only in the last message if needed.
