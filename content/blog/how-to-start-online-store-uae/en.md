**Short answer:** to start an online store in the UAE, choose your product and market, get an ecommerce licence, open a business bank account, pick a platform such as Shopify, connect payments and shipping, then launch and market the store. Most small businesses can complete these steps in 3–6 weeks.

## Step 1: Choose your product and market

Before the licence or the design, answer three questions:

- **What will you sell?** Something you make, wholesale stock, or products shipped by suppliers with no inventory (dropshipping).
- **Who will you sell to?** The UAE only, or the whole GCC? This decides your shipping, currency and languages.
- **Why will they buy from you?** Price, quality, fast delivery, or the story behind the brand.

In the UAE, fashion, perfume, beauty and electronics are among the biggest online categories, which also makes them the most competitive. You win there through brand and experience, not just price.

## Step 2: Get an ecommerce licence

Selling online without a licence is a violation, and most payment gateways won't onboard you without one. Common routes:

- **The Dubai e-Trader licence:** for UAE residents, roughly AED 1,370 a year.
- **A mainland ecommerce licence** through your emirate's economic department.
- **A free-zone company** that permits ecommerce activity.

We compare them in our guide to the [ecommerce licence in the UAE](/en/blog/ecommerce-license-uae).

## Step 3: Open a business bank account

Payment gateways pay out to an account in the licence holder's name. Have your licence, Emirates ID and, for companies, the memorandum of association ready. Some digital banks open accounts faster than traditional ones, so compare before deciding.

## Step 4: Choose your ecommerce platform

The main options in the region:

- **Shopify:** strongest for custom design, selling across countries and global ad integrations.
- **Salla and Zid:** strong Saudi platforms for the local market, with more limited design freedom.
- **WooCommerce:** flexible, but hosting, security and maintenance are on you.

If you're torn between the two most popular choices, read [Shopify vs Salla](/en/blog/shopify-vs-salla).

## Step 5: Design the store, in Arabic as well

Gulf shoppers read Arabic first, and a store that looks machine-translated loses trust fast. Focus on:

- Correct **RTL** layout across every page, not just the text.
- A clear product page: real photos, price in AED, delivery time and return policy.
- Mobile-first design, since most orders come from phones.

More in our guide to building an [Arabic Shopify store](/en/blog/arabic-shopify-store-rtl).

## Step 6: Connect payments

UAE shoppers expect cards and Apple Pay, instalments through Tabby or Tamara, and sometimes cash on delivery. Because Shopify Payments isn't available in the UAE, you'll need a third-party gateway. See [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

## Step 7: Set up shipping

You have two models:

1. **Ship it yourself** with a courier such as Aramex, Jeebly or Quiqup, packing orders from home or your own warehouse.
2. **Use a fulfillment centre** that stores, packs and ships your products automatically when an order comes in.

We compare the options in [Shopify shipping in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-shipping-uae).

## Step 8: Launch and get your first visitors

Before launch, place a full real test order: payment, delivery and a return. Then:

- Install the Meta, TikTok, Snapchat and Google **pixels** before your first ad.
- Start with a small ad budget and test products and messages.
- Publish pages and articles that answer your customers' questions, so Google sends you free traffic. See [Shopify SEO in Arabic](/en/blog/shopify-seo-arabic).

## How long does it all take?

| Stage | Typical time |
|---|---|
| Licence | 1 day to 2 weeks, depending on type |
| Bank account | Days to several weeks |
| Store design | 5–7 business days with a specialist agency |
| Payment gateway approval | 1–3 weeks |
| Shipping setup | Days to a week |

The smart move is to run stages in parallel: design the store while the bank and the gateway review your application.

## FAQ

### Can I run an online store in the UAE without a licence?
No. Any business activity in the UAE, even fully online, needs a valid licence. Trading without one can lead to fines and closed accounts.

### How much does it cost to start an online store in the UAE?
For a small, professional Shopify store, about AED 4,700–5,500 in the first year before ads and stock. See the full breakdown in [Shopify store cost in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-store-cost-uae).

### Can I sell to other GCC countries from a UAE store?
Yes. Shopify supports multiple currencies and languages, but you need to plan international shipping, customs and returns for each country.

### Do I need to register for VAT?
Registration is mandatory once taxable sales exceed AED 375,000 over 12 months, and optional from AED 187,500. Confirm the details with the Federal Tax Authority or a certified accountant.
