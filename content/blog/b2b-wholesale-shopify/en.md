**Short answer:** you can sell **wholesale (B2B)** from the same Shopify store you use for consumers. Since **April 2026**, core B2B features are available on the **Basic, Grow and Advanced** plans: company accounts, volume pricing, quantity rules and payment terms, with limits such as **a maximum of 3 active catalogs**. If you need **unlimited catalogs** and custom pricing per customer at scale, that's part of **Shopify Plus**.

> Features and limits change. Confirm current details in Shopify's documentation.

## Why sell wholesale online?

- **Less time:** instead of orders over WhatsApp, email and spreadsheets.
- **Easy repeat orders:** business customers order the same list every month.
- **Unified inventory** across consumer and wholesale sales.
- **Professionalism** that helps you win bigger business customers.

## Core B2B features

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Company accounts | A company with several locations and buyers |
| Catalogs | Custom prices for a group of customers |
| Volume pricing | Lower prices as quantity increases |
| Quantity rules | Minimums and increments (such as cases of 12) |
| Payment terms | For example, pay within 30 days |
| Vaulted cards | Easy payment for repeat orders |

## Standard plans or Plus?

**Standard plans are enough if:**
- You have few price groups (up to 3 catalogs).
- You have a limited number of business customers.

**You need Plus if:**
- You need **many catalogs** or a custom price per customer.
- You need **partial payments** or deposits.
- Your wholesale operation is large and complex.

See [when you need Shopify Plus](/en/blog/shopify-plus-uae).

## Practical setup

1. **Identify wholesale customers** and create company accounts for them.
2. **Create catalogs** with custom prices for each group.
3. **Quantity rules:** minimums and increments.
4. **Payment terms** for trusted customers.
5. **A wholesale application page** where customers apply and you review.
6. **VAT and invoices:** correct tax invoices for businesses. See [VAT for online stores](/en/blog/vat-for-online-stores-uae).

## The business buyer's experience

- **Quick ordering:** a product list with quantity fields instead of browsing product by product.
- **One-click reorders** from past orders.
- **Prices visible only after login** for wholesale customers.
- **Arabic and English** for customers of different nationalities. See [bilingual Shopify store](/en/blog/bilingual-shopify-store).

## Wholesale shipping

Large orders need different shipping: pallets, weight-based rates or warehouse pickup. Agree special arrangements with your courier or fulfillment partner. See [ecommerce fulfillment](/en/blog/fulfillment-centers-uae).

## FAQ

### Do I need Shopify Plus to sell wholesale?
No. Core features have been available on standard plans since April 2026. Plus is for unlimited catalogs or advanced features.

### Can I sell to consumers and wholesale from one store?
Yes, and that's one of the biggest advantages of Shopify B2B: one inventory and one admin.

### How do I hide wholesale prices from consumers?
Custom prices show only to company accounts after they log in.
