**Short answer:** in the Gulf, most store visits and orders come from **mobile**. That means designing **mobile-first**, then desktop, not the other way round. Focus on **load speed**, **one-thumb menus and search**, a **product page** that shows everything important on the first screen, a **sticky buy button**, **fast checkout** with Apple Pay, and **readable Arabic** at a comfortable size.

## Why "mobile first"?

- Ads on Snapchat, TikTok and Instagram send shoppers **straight from their phones**.
- People shop in short moments: on breaks, after iftar, before bed.
- Google evaluates your site **using the mobile version**.

A store that looks great on desktop and frustrating on mobile loses most of its customers.

## The elements that matter

### 1. A simple header
Logo, menu button, search and cart. Don't crowd it.

### 2. An easy menu
- Opens from the **right** on the Arabic version.
- Main collections only, with small collection images if it suits.
- The language switcher inside the menu.

### 3. Excellent search
Search that suggests products as you type and handles typos and both Arabic and English.

### 4. A comfortable collection page
- **Two products per row** usually works better than one large one.
- Filters in a bottom sheet.
- Price and instalments visible on the product card.

### 5. A compact product page
Photos, price, instalments, variants, delivery and buy button on the first screen. See [product page design](/en/blog/product-page-design).

### 6. A cart drawer
The cart opens as a side drawer instead of a new page, with a clear checkout button and "AED … away from free shipping".

### 7. Fast checkout
**Apple Pay** first on iPhone, then cards and instalments. Every extra checkout field lowers conversion. See [Apple Pay on Shopify in the UAE](/en/blog/apple-pay-shopify-uae).

### 8. Thumb-sized buttons
Buttons and links big enough to tap, with enough space between them.

### 9. Readable Arabic
A clear Arabic font of at least 16px for body text, with comfortable line height. See [Arabic Shopify store](/en/blog/arabic-shopify-store-rtl).

### 10. A WhatsApp button
A sticky button that doesn't cover the buy button.

## Test on a real phone

A desktop emulator isn't enough. Open your store on:
- iPhone and Android.
- Small and large screens.
- A mobile network (not just fast Wi-Fi).

And run a full journey: ad, product, cart, checkout.

## Speed on mobile

Mobile suffers most from slowness, and every second of delay loses visitors. See [Shopify store speed](/en/blog/shopify-store-speed).

## FAQ

### Should I build a separate mobile version?
No. Responsive design works on every screen, but it's planned mobile-first.

### What font size works on mobile?
At least 16px for body text, and slightly larger for Arabic.

### What matters most in a mobile store?
Speed, clear price and delivery information, and easy checkout.
