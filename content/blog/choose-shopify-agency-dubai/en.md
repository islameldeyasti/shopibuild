**Short answer:** the right Shopify agency in Dubai can show you live stores it built for Gulf brands, builds Arabic into the theme rather than bolting on a translation app, gives you a fixed written price and timeline, and hands over the store and every account in your name. Ask the ten questions below before you pay anything.

## Why the choice matters so much

A store is more than a design. It's the checkout customers trust, the tracking your ads depend on, and the speed that affects your Google rankings. The wrong choice costs you twice: once for the project, and again when you rebuild a year later.

## 10 questions to ask any agency

### 1. Show me live stores in the Gulf
Links to stores that are live today, not mock-ups. Test them on your phone: speed, Arabic, and the checkout.

### 2. How do you build the Arabic version?
The right answer: Arabic is built into the theme, with correct RTL in menus, sliders, filters and checkout. The weak answer: "we install a translation app". More on the difference in our guide to the [Arabic Shopify store](/en/blog/arabic-shopify-store-rtl).

### 3. Is the price fixed, and what exactly does it include?
Get a written quote covering the number of pages, products uploaded, integrations, revision rounds and post-launch support. Open-ended hourly billing on a store project usually ends up higher than expected.

### 4. How long will it take, and when does the clock start?
A specialist agency can deliver a professional store in business days, not months, as long as your content is ready. Ask when the timeline starts and what usually causes delays.

### 5. Are the accounts in my name?
The Shopify store, domain, ad accounts and payment gateway must all be owned by you. The agency should work as a collaborator only.

### 6. How do you set up payments in the UAE?
Shopify Payments isn't available in the UAE, so the agency must know the local gateways and how to connect Tabby, Tamara and Apple Pay. See [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

### 7. Do you connect shipping and fulfillment?
Ask about their experience with local couriers and fulfillment centres, and whether orders reach the warehouse automatically or by hand.

### 8. How many apps will my store need?
Every app is a monthly fee and a source of slowdown. A good agency builds core features into the theme and keeps apps to a minimum.

### 9. Do you install ad tracking?
Meta, TikTok, Snapchat and Google pixels, with server-side events (CAPI). Without proper tracking, your ads run blind.

### 10. What happens after launch?
How many months of support? Is there training on running the store? Who fixes things if something breaks two months later?

## Red flags

- They can't show a single live Arabic store.
- A very low price with no written scope.
- They want the store or domain registered in their name.
- They rely on apps for translation and RTL instead of design.
- They never mention speed, tracking or SEO.
- They ask for the full amount upfront before any design.

## Agency or freelancer?

| | Freelancer | Specialist agency |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Usually lower | Higher, but fixed and clear |
| Scope | Design and edits | Design, integrations, tracking, SEO |
| Continuity | Depends on one person | A team and post-launch support |
| Timeline | Varies a lot | Clearer and written down |
| Best for | Small edits to an existing store | Launching a new store or a rebuild |

A freelancer is a great choice for small edits. But if you're launching a brand and spending on ads, integrations, tracking and support make a big difference.

## FAQ

### Which is the best Shopify agency in Dubai?
There's no single best for everyone. The best one for you has live Gulf stores similar to your project, a fixed price, a written scope and post-launch support.

### Should I work with a Shopify Partner agency?
Being listed in the Shopify Partner Directory helps, because you can read real client reviews. But past work and local integrations matter more.

### How long does an agency take to build a Shopify store?
5–7 business days for a clearly scoped store with content ready, and weeks for large or fully custom projects.

### Can I edit the store myself after handover?
You should be able to. Ask for a theme where you can edit text, images and sections in the Shopify editor, along with training videos.
