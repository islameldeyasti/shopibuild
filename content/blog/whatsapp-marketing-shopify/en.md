**Short answer:** WhatsApp is **the main communication channel** for Gulf shoppers. Use it in four places: a **WhatsApp button** for pre-purchase questions, **order and shipping notifications** instead of email, **abandoned cart recovery**, and **campaigns** to customers who opted in. To automate at scale you need the **WhatsApp Business API** through an approved provider, and **customer consent** before marketing messages.

## Why WhatsApp?

Gulf shoppers:
- Open WhatsApp far more than email.
- Ask before buying: "Do you have size M?", "When will it arrive?"
- Trust a store they can talk to.

A store without WhatsApp loses questions that could have become sales.

## 1. A WhatsApp button in your store

- A sticky button on mobile and on product pages.
- A **pre-filled message** with the product name: "Hi, I have a question about…".
- Fast replies, ideally within minutes during working hours.
- An automatic greeting outside working hours.

## 2. Order and shipping notifications

Replace or supplement email with WhatsApp messages:
- **Order confirmed**, with its number and value.
- **Shipped**, with a tracking link.
- **Out for delivery** on the day.
- **Delivered**, with a review request.

This cuts "where's my order?" questions and builds trust. For cash on delivery, **order confirmation** over WhatsApp reduces refused deliveries. See [cash on delivery on Shopify](/en/blog/cash-on-delivery-shopify).

## 3. Abandoned cart recovery

A WhatsApp message to a shopper who left their cart is usually read more than an email. For example:

> "Hi Sara, we saw you picked the Taif rose perfume. It's still reserved for you; complete your order here: [link]. Delivered within a day in Dubai."

On one condition: the shopper has **agreed to be contacted** on WhatsApp.

## 4. Campaigns and offers

- Seasonal offers (Ramadan, Eid, White Friday). See [Ramadan and White Friday sales](/en/blog/ramadan-white-friday-sales).
- New product launches for interested customers.
- Exclusive offers for repeat customers.

**The golden rule:** few, valuable messages. Too many messages get you blocked, which harms your account.

## WhatsApp Business App or API?

| | WhatsApp Business app | WhatsApp Business API |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Small store, manual chats | Automation, campaigns, high volume |
| Automation | Limited (quick replies) | Full, with Shopify |
| Team | Limited | A full team on one number |
| Cost | Free | Provider fees + Meta fees by message type |
| Shopify connection | Manual | Automatic through provider apps |

The API requires an **approved provider** and an app that connects it to Shopify. Meta charges per message by type (marketing, utility, authentication), with prices that vary by country and change over time.

## Consent and privacy

- Get **clear consent** before marketing messages, such as a checkbox at checkout: "Send me offers on WhatsApp".
- Give customers an easy way to **opt out**.
- Explain how phone numbers are used in your **privacy policy**.

## FAQ

### Can I send marketing messages to all my customers on WhatsApp?
Only to customers who opted in. Messaging without consent puts your number at risk of being banned.

### How much does the WhatsApp Business API cost?
Provider fees, plus Meta's per-message fees by type and recipient country. Ask your provider for pricing.

### Is WhatsApp better than email?
In the Gulf, usually yes for notifications and quick communication. Email remains useful for details and invoices.
