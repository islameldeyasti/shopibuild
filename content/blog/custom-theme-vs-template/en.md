**Short answer:** a **ready-made template** is cheaper and faster to start, and fine for testing an idea. A **custom theme** is worth it when **your brand depends on visual identity**, you need **proper Arabic** that templates don't deliver, or you want **more speed and fewer apps**. Today the difference isn't necessarily price: a clearly scoped custom theme can cost close to a paid template plus its modifications.

## What's the actual difference?

- **Template:** a theme from the Shopify Theme Store that you customise through settings (colours, fonts, images). Thousands of stores share the same structure.
- **Custom theme:** a theme built or rebuilt for your brand, with its own design, structure and features.

## Comparison

| | Template | Custom theme |
|---|---|---|
| Upfront cost | Low ($0–$400 + modifications) | Higher, depending on scope |
| Time to launch | Fast | Days to weeks |
| Distinctiveness | Looks like many stores | A unique identity |
| Arabic and RTL | Varies, usually needs fixes | Built right from the start |
| Speed | Depends on theme and apps | Built for speed |
| Apps | Often many, to fill gaps | Features built into the theme |
| Control | Within the template's limits | Complete |

## The hidden cost of a template

The template is cheap, but you usually add:
- **RTL and Arabic fixes**.
- **Apps** for missing features: filters, reviews, WhatsApp buttons, bundles. Each one is a monthly fee.
- **A developer** to fix conflicts between apps.

After a year, the total can exceed the cost of a custom theme. See [Shopify store cost in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-store-cost-uae).

## When a template is enough

- You're testing a new product idea on a limited budget.
- Your products don't rely heavily on visual identity.
- You found a template with proper Arabic support. See [best Shopify themes for Arabic](/en/blog/best-shopify-themes-arabic).

## When you need a custom theme

- A **premium or story-driven brand** (perfume, fashion, abayas).
- **Arabic** is central for your customers, and you want it as good as English.
- You spend a **large ad budget**, so every 0.5% gain in conversion matters.
- You want to **cut apps** and speed up the store.
- You want to **stand out** from competitors using the same templates.

### How much does a custom Shopify theme cost in the UAE?

### Does a custom theme take longer to launch?
With a specialist team and a clear scope, the difference is only a few days.

You should be able to. Ask for a theme built with sections you can edit in the Shopify editor without code.

You can, but moving later means redoing work. If you're confident in your brand, start right from the beginning.
