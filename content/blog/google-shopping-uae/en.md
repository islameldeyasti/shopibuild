**Short answer:** you can show your products in **Google's Shopping tab** in two ways: **free listings** (the UAE is a supported country) and **paid Shopping ads** at the top of results. You connect them through the **Google & YouTube** app in Shopify, linked to **Google Merchant Center**. Success depends on **product data quality**: titles, images, prices and availability.

## Why Google Shopping?

A shopper searching "Cambodian oud oil 12 ml" is **ready to buy**. Shopping ads show them your product image, price and store name right in the results. That's high-intent traffic, unlike social ads that rely on discovery.

## Step 1: connect Shopify to Merchant Center

1. Install the **Google & YouTube** app in Shopify.
2. Connect your Google account and create or link **Merchant Center**.
3. Set the **country** to the UAE, along with language and currency.
4. Complete the store requirements: return policy, shipping, contact details and a secure checkout.
5. The app syncs your products automatically and keeps them updated.

## Step 2: free listings

In the app settings, make sure **Free listings** is enabled as a destination for your products. Your products can then appear at no cost in the Shopping tab, Google Images and elsewhere, subject to eligibility.

## Step 3: paid Shopping ads

- Create a **Performance Max** or **Shopping** campaign in Google Ads.
- Start with **best-sellers** that have good margins.
- Turn on **conversion tracking** from the app, with enhanced conversions.
- Monitor **return on ad spend** per product, and pause the losers.

## Optimising product data (the most important part)

| Element | Advice |
|---|---|
| Title | Brand + product type + key attribute + size, e.g. "Cambodian oud oil 12 ml" |
| Image | White or light background, with no text or logos over the product |
| Price | Exactly matches the price in your store |
| Availability | Updated automatically |
| Description | Clear, using the words shoppers search for |
| Identifiers | Barcode (GTIN) where available, or brand and MPN |
| Category | The right Google product category |

**Common mistake:** a price mismatch between the ad and the store, or an image with promotional text. Both cause **product disapprovals** in Merchant Center.

## Arabic and English

You can submit products in more than one language. Arabic product titles help you reach Arabic searchers, which are often less competitive results. See [Shopify SEO in Arabic](/en/blog/shopify-seo-arabic).

## Common problems

- **Disapproved products:** check the diagnostics in Merchant Center (prices, images, policies).
- **Suspended account:** usually due to missing store information or policies.
- **No impressions:** check country, language and that free listings are enabled.

## FAQ

### Are Google free listings available in the UAE?
Yes, the UAE is a supported country. Confirm eligibility in your store's Google & YouTube app settings.

### What budget should I start with for Shopping ads?
Enough to collect data on your strongest products over a few weeks, then scale the winners.

### Do I need barcodes for my products?
Not always. For products without barcodes, use the brand and manufacturer part number.
