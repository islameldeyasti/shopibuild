**Short answer:** in the Gulf, WhatsApp is stronger for quick communication, but **email is a cheap channel you own**, ideal for **automated flows** (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back), **seasonal campaigns** and **invoices and details**. Start with **4 core automations**, write them in Arabic and English, and only email people who **opted in**.

## Why email still matters

- **You own it:** your email list isn't affected by platform algorithm changes.
- **Cheap:** the cost per message is low compared with ads.
- **Automated:** flows run on their own once set up.
- **Complements WhatsApp:** WhatsApp for quick alerts, email for detail, images and longer offers. See [WhatsApp marketing](/en/blog/whatsapp-marketing-shopify).

## Building your list

- **A sign-up pop-up** with a simple offer (such as free shipping on the first order), appearing after a few seconds, not instantly.
- **A consent checkbox** at checkout.
- **A "join our list" page** for exclusive launches.
- Never buy email lists.

## The four core automations

| Email | When | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | After sign-up | Introduce the brand + first-order offer |
| Abandoned cart | 1 hour to 1 day later | Bring the shopper back to complete the order |
| Post-purchase | After delivery | Review request + usage tips + complementary products |
| Win-back | 2–3 months with no purchase | An offer or news to bring the customer back |

Abandoned cart details are in [abandoned cart recovery](/en/blog/abandoned-cart-recovery).

## Campaigns

- **Seasons:** Ramadan, Eid and White Friday. See [Gulf ecommerce seasons](/en/blog/ramadan-white-friday-sales).
- **Launches:** new products to subscribers first.
- **Content:** useful blog articles. See [ecommerce blog strategy](/en/blog/ecommerce-blog-strategy).

**Cadence:** one or two campaigns a month to start, more in peak seasons.

## Writing in Arabic

- **A short, clear subject line.**
- **Correct RTL** in the email template (many templates break in Arabic).
- **Email each customer in their language**: Arabic or English, based on their store preference.
- **One clear button** instead of many links.
- **Light images**, since many people read on mobile.

## Tools

- **Shopify Email** and built-in automations: an easy, integrated start.
- **Specialist tools** such as Klaviyo when you need deeper segmentation and automation.

## Measure results

- **Click rate** and **sales per email** matter more than open rate (now less accurate due to privacy changes).
- **Unsubscribes**: if high, send less often or improve the content.

## FAQ

### Does email work in the Gulf?
Yes, especially for automations, invoices and offers. It works best alongside WhatsApp, not instead of it.

### How many emails should I send a month?
Start with one or two campaigns a month plus automations, and increase in peak seasons.

### Do I need a paid tool?
To start, Shopify's built-in tools are enough. Specialist tools help as your list grows.
