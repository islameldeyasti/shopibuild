**Short answer:** you can sell from your UAE store to every GCC country. You need **a courier that covers the Gulf** (such as Aramex, DHL or others), **markets and currencies set up** in Shopify for each country, **complete clarity on customs and taxes** and who pays them, **honest delivery times**, and a **practical return policy** for international orders.

## Why sell to the GCC?

The UAE is a strong market, but **Saudi Arabia** is the Gulf's largest ecommerce market, and Kuwait and Qatar have high purchasing power. Many UAE brands multiply their sales when they open shipping to the GCC.

## Step 1: choose a courier

| Courier | Note |
|---|---|
| Aramex | Wide GCC coverage and ecommerce experience |
| DHL, FedEx and UPS | Fast, usually at a higher cost |
| Emirates Post | An economical option depending on destination |
| Regional fulfillment centres | Some have warehouses in several countries |

Get weight-based rates for each country, and check the **courier's Shopify integration**.

## Step 2: markets and currencies in Shopify

In Shopify you can set up a **market** for each country or group of countries:
- **Currency:** Saudi riyal for Saudi Arabia, dinar for Kuwait, and so on.
- **Prices:** automatic conversion, or fixed prices per market.
- **Language:** Arabic and English.
- **Shipping:** different rates and times per country.

A shopper in Riyadh sees prices in riyals, which builds trust and conversion.

## Step 3: customs and taxes

When shipping to another country:
- **Customs duties** and **import taxes** may apply, depending on country, product and value.
- Each shipment needs a **commercial invoice**.
- Some products have **restrictions** in certain countries (such as some cosmetics and alcohol-based perfumes).

**Most important:** tell shoppers before they pay **who pays the duties**: you (duties-included shipping) or the customer on delivery. A surprise at the door means a refused delivery.

> Customs and tax rules differ across GCC countries and change. Confirm with your courier and each country's official authorities.

## Step 4: delivery times

Show delivery times per country on product and shipping pages, for example "Saudi Arabia: 3–5 business days". Honesty here beats a promise you can't keep.

## Step 5: international returns

Returns from another country are expensive. Options:
- **Exchanges** instead of refunds.
- **Store credit** instead of cash refunds.
- Clear rules on **who pays return shipping**.

## Payments for GCC shoppers

Tabby and Tamara also operate in Saudi Arabia and Kuwait, and cards and Apple Pay run through your gateway. Check that your gateway accepts **cards issued in other GCC countries** and at what fee. See [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

## When do you need stock in Saudi Arabia?

Once Saudi orders become a large share of sales, holding part of your stock inside Saudi Arabia through a local fulfillment provider cuts delivery time, cost and customs per order. See [ecommerce fulfillment in the UAE](/en/blog/fulfillment-centers-uae).

## FAQ

### Can I sell to Saudi Arabia from a UAE store?
Yes. You need international shipping, clarity on customs, and a check of any requirements for your product category.

### Who pays customs duties?
You decide: either include them in shipping, or the customer pays on delivery. What matters is saying so before payment.

### How long does shipping from the UAE to Saudi Arabia take?
It depends on the courier and the city. Ask your courier for actual times per city.

### Can Shopify show prices in each country's currency?
Yes, through Markets, with automatic conversion or fixed prices per market.
