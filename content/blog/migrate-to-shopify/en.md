**Short answer:** migrating to Shopify has five stages: **move the data** (products, customers, orders), **rebuild the design**, **redirect old URLs to new ones** (301 redirects) so you keep your Google rankings, **connect payments, shipping and tracking**, then **switch the domain**. The step people get wrong most is **redirects**, and skipping them can wipe out years of SEO.

## Why merchants move to Shopify

- They want **custom design** that sets them apart.
- They sell, or plan to sell, **in several countries**.
- They're tired of WooCommerce **maintenance**, plugins and hosting.
- They need more accurate **ad tracking**.

See the comparisons: [Shopify vs Salla](/en/blog/shopify-vs-salla), [Shopify vs Zid](/en/blog/shopify-vs-zid) and [Shopify vs WooCommerce](/en/blog/shopify-vs-woocommerce-uae).

## What transfers and what gets rebuilt?

| Item | Transfers? | How |
|---|---|---|
| Products, images and prices | Yes | CSV or a migration tool |
| Variants (sizes, colours) | Yes | Needs careful review |
| Customers | Yes | CSV (passwords don't transfer) |
| Past orders | Usually | Migration tool |
| Reviews | Usually | A reviews app with import |
| Design | No | Rebuilt |
| Pages and articles | Yes | Copy or migration tool |
| Apps and plugins | No | Shopify alternatives, or built into the theme |

## Stage 1: inventory and planning

Before moving anything:
- **Export every URL on your current site**: products, categories, pages and articles.
- **Identify the pages that get Google traffic** in Google Search Console.
- **List the apps you rely on**, and an alternative for each.

## Stage 2: move the data

- Move products first and review **variants, images and descriptions** in Arabic and English.
- Move customers. Note: **passwords don't transfer**, so customers need to reactivate their accounts. Prepare a friendly message for them.
- Move past orders if you need the history.

## Stage 3: build the store

This is your chance to build a better store than before: proper Arabic, faster pages, fewer apps. See [Arabic Shopify store](/en/blog/arabic-shopify-store-rtl).

## Stage 4: redirects (most important for SEO)

Every old URL needs a **301** redirect to its new equivalent:

| Old URL | New URL |
|---|---|
| /product/oud-oil-12ml | /products/oud-oil-12ml |
| /category/perfumes | /collections/perfumes |
| /blog/oud-guide | /blogs/news/oud-guide |

In Shopify: **Content → Menus → URL Redirects**, where you can upload them as a CSV.

**Without redirects:** old URLs in Google return "page not found", and your rankings collapse.

## Stage 5: payments, shipping and tracking

- Connect your gateway, Tabby and Tamara. See [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).
- Connect shipping and fulfillment.
- Install ad pixels before launch. See [Meta Pixel and CAPI on Shopify](/en/blog/meta-pixel-capi-shopify).

## Stage 6: launch and switch the domain

- Pick a quiet time, not mid-campaign or mid-season.
- Point the domain to Shopify.
- Submit the **new sitemap** in Search Console.
- Watch 404 errors for two weeks and add any missing redirects.

## Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] All products and variants moved and reviewed
- [ ] Every important old URL has a 301 redirect
- [ ] A successful test order with every payment method
- [ ] Shipping connected and sending tracking numbers
- [ ] Pixels recording events correctly
- [ ] Meta titles and descriptions carried over
- [ ] A message to customers about the new store

## FAQ

### Will I lose my Google rankings when moving to Shopify?
There may be a temporary dip, but with correct 301 redirects and preserved content, rankings usually recover within weeks.

### How long does a migration take?
It depends on product count and complexity. A small to medium store can move in one to two weeks, including design.

### Do customer accounts and passwords transfer?
Customer data transfers, but passwords don't. Customers reactivate their accounts through a link.
