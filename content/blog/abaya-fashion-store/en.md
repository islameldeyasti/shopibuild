**Short answer:** a successful abaya store solves two problems: **"Will it fit me?"** and **"Is the fabric like the photo?"** The answer: **an accurate size guide**, **real photos and video on a model** with her size and height stated, **a clear fabric description**, a **made-to-measure option** if you offer it, and **an easy exchange policy**. With Ramadan and both Eids, prepare stock and the store weeks ahead.

## Why abayas are a strong category

- Steady demand all year, with a clear peak before Ramadan and both Eids.
- Customers buy several pieces a year for different occasions.
- Lots of room to stand out through design, embroidery and fabric.

## Sizing: the most important page in your store

- **A size chart in centimetres:** length, chest width, sleeve length.
- **Choosing advice:** "Between two sizes? Choose the larger one."
- **The model's size and height** under every photo: "Model is 168 cm and wears size 54."
- **Custom length:** a length field on the product page.
- **A WhatsApp button** for sizing advice.

## Photos and video

- Photos **on a model**, from front, back and side.
- **Close-ups** of fabric and embroidery.
- **A movement video** showing how the fabric falls.
- **Colours as accurate as possible**: colour mismatch is one of the biggest causes of returns.

## Fabric and care

State clearly: fabric type (crepe, nida, linen, chiffon), weight, sheerness, and how to wash and iron. See [product page design](/en/blog/product-page-design).

## Made to measure

If you offer tailoring:
- Fields on the product page: length, sleeve width, notes.
- **A clear production time**: "Made to measure takes 5 business days."
- **A different exchange policy** for tailored pieces, shown before payment.

## Filters and collections

- Filters: size, colour, fabric, occasion (everyday, formal, Ramadan), price.
- Collections by **occasion** and **collection**, not just by type.

## Reducing returns

- An accurate size guide and real photos.
- **Free size exchanges** instead of refunds.
- Reviews that mention size: "I took 56, I'm 165 cm and it fits perfectly."
- See [return and refund policy](/en/blog/returns-policy-uae).

## Payments and seasons

- Enable **Tabby and Tamara**, which are very popular in fashion. See [Tabby and Tamara on Shopify](/en/blog/tabby-tamara-shopify).
- **6–8 weeks before Ramadan:** a Ramadan collection, a dedicated page and ads. See [Gulf ecommerce seasons](/en/blog/ramadan-white-friday-sales).
- Show **the last order date** for delivery before Eid clearly.

## Marketing

- "Styling looks" content on Instagram and TikTok.
- Collaborations with permit-holding influencers. See [influencer marketing in the UAE](/en/blog/influencer-marketing-uae).
- Launch new collections to customers on WhatsApp first.

## FAQ

### How do I reduce size-related returns?
A size chart in centimetres, the model's size and height, reviews that mention size, and a custom length option.

### Should abayas be shown on a model or laid flat?
Both, but model photos and video sell more because they show how the fabric falls.

### When should I prepare a Ramadan collection?
At least 6–8 weeks before Ramadan. Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin around 8 February.
