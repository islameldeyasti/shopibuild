**Short answer:** a **Shopify agency** builds you a store that's ready to sell: **design** in your brand, **development** of the theme and features, **payments and shipping** connected, **ad tracking** installed, **SEO** set up, and **training and support** after launch. You need an agency when you want a professional store quickly and without costly mistakes, especially in Arabic. You can start alone if your budget is very tight and you're testing a small idea.

## What a Shopify agency does, in detail

### 1. Strategy
Understanding your product, market and customers, and choosing the right package, platform setup and integrations.

### 2. Design
A visual identity for the store, mobile-first design, and product pages that sell. See [product page design](/en/blog/product-page-design).

### 3. Development
A custom or modified theme, features built in instead of apps, and Arabic with RTL. See [Shopify development](/en/shopify-development).

### 4. Payments and shipping
Gateways, Tabby, Tamara and Apple Pay, couriers and fulfillment.

### 5. Tracking
Meta, TikTok, Snapchat and Google pixels with server-side tracking. See [Meta Pixel and CAPI on Shopify](/en/blog/meta-pixel-capi-shopify).

### 6. SEO and GEO
The technical foundation, keywords and content. See [Shopify SEO services](/en/shopify-seo-service).

### 7. Launch and support
Testing everything, training, and support after launch.

## An agency, or build it yourself?

| | Build it yourself | Specialist agency |
|---|---|---|
| Direct cost | Lower | Higher |
| Time | Weeks of your own time | Business days |
| Arabic and RTL | Hard without experience | Built properly |
| Payments and tracking | Trial and error | Connected and tested |
| Costly mistakes | Likely | Fewer |
| Best for | Testing a small idea | Launching a serious brand |

**The real calculation:** your time has value, and mistakes in payments and tracking cost sales and wasted ad spend.

## Agency or freelancer?

A freelancer is a good choice for defined tasks. An agency gives you a team, a written scope, a fixed price and ongoing support. Details in [how to choose a Shopify agency in Dubai](/en/blog/choose-shopify-agency-dubai).

## What to ask any agency before signing

- Live stores in the Gulf.
- A written proposal with scope, price and timeline.
- Every account in your name.
- How they build Arabic.
- The post-launch support plan.

## FAQ

### How much does a Shopify agency cost in the UAE?
From around AED 2,000 for a professional store with a clear scope, up to tens of thousands for large projects. See [Shopify store cost](/en/blog/shopify-store-cost-uae).

### Does the agency run the store after launch?
It depends on the agreement. Most agencies offer technical support, and some offer marketing and SEO management on a retainer.

### Does the agency need to be in my city?
No. Most of the work happens online; what matters is their experience with your market and language.
