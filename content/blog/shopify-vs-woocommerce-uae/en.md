**Short answer:** **WooCommerce** is a free plugin and extremely flexible, but hosting, security, updates and speed are **your responsibility**. **Shopify** costs a monthly subscription but handles all of that and gives you a trusted checkout. For most UAE stores that want to focus on selling rather than maintenance, **Shopify is cheaper in total cost and lower risk**. WooCommerce suits teams with in-house developers or highly custom needs.

## The core difference

- **Shopify** is a hosted platform: you pay a subscription, and it takes care of servers, security, updates and checkout.
- **WooCommerce** is a WordPress plugin: the software is free, but you need hosting, an SSL certificate, backups, ongoing updates and usually several paid plugins.

## Side by side

| Factor | Shopify | WooCommerce |
|---|---|---|
| Software cost | Monthly subscription | Free |
| Hosting | Included | On you (cheap to expensive) |
| Security and updates | Included | On you |
| Speed | Good by default, depends on theme | Depends on hosting and plugins |
| Checkout | Trusted and stable | Depends on plugins |
| Arabic and RTL | Needs a properly built theme | WordPress supports RTL; quality depends on theme |
| Flexibility | High within Shopify's system | Nearly unlimited |
| Maintenance | Low | Ongoing |

## The true cost: free doesn't mean cheap

WooCommerce itself is free, but a professional store on it usually needs:

- Solid hosting that can handle traffic (cheap for a small store, expensive for a large one).
- A paid theme and paid plugins: filters, subscriptions, shipping, invoices, security.
- A developer for maintenance, updates and fixes.

On Shopify, most of this is in the subscription. The real difference shows in **years two and three**, when WooCommerce maintenance and update costs kick in.

Compare with [Shopify store cost in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-store-cost-uae).

## Security

Checkout is the most important page in your store. On Shopify, security and card-data standards (PCI) are the platform's responsibility. On WooCommerce, an outdated plugin or a hosting vulnerability can put your store at risk, and monitoring is on you.

## Payments in the UAE

- **Shopify:** external gateways, since Shopify Payments isn't available in the UAE, plus a Shopify transaction fee. See [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).
- **WooCommerce:** no platform transaction fee, and many local gateways offer WooCommerce plugins.

That's a point for WooCommerce for very high-volume stores, though it's usually offset by maintenance costs.

## Choose WooCommerce if…

- You already have a strong WordPress site with lots of content and want to add a store.
- You have an in-house team or a permanent developer.
- You need unusual customisations Shopify can't support.

## Choose Shopify if…

- You want to focus on selling and marketing, not servers and updates.
- You want a trusted checkout and guaranteed security.
- You plan to grow and sell to several countries.
- You don't have a permanent technical team.

## FAQ

### Which is cheaper, Shopify or WooCommerce?
WooCommerce is usually cheaper at the start. With solid hosting, plugins and maintenance, the gap narrows or flips in Shopify's favour over time.

### Which is better for SEO?
Both are strong. WooCommerce gives more control; Shopify gives a clean, fast base with less effort. See [Shopify SEO in Arabic](/en/blog/shopify-seo-arabic).

### Can I move from WooCommerce to Shopify?
Yes. You migrate products, customers and orders, and redirect old URLs to keep your rankings.

### Which is easier to run day to day?
Shopify, especially for owners without technical experience.
