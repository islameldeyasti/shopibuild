**Short answer:** conversion rises when you **remove the reasons shoppers hesitate**. In the Gulf, the biggest are **weak trust**, **unclear delivery and final price**, **limited payment options** (Apple Pay and instalments), **a slow mobile store** and **a poorly translated Arabic store**. Fix these first, then test the details.

## What is conversion rate?

The share of visitors who buy. For example, 100 visitors and 2 orders = 2%. "Normal" varies widely by category and traffic source, so compare your store **with itself** over time rather than with general benchmarks.

## Where do shoppers drop off?

Open your Shopify reports and look at the **purchase funnel**:
- Page view → **add to cart**: if weak, the problem is the product page.
- Cart → **checkout started**: the problem is the cart or shipping cost.
- Checkout → **order completed**: the problem is payment options or a price surprise.

## 15 fixes

### Trust
1. **A professional design with a clear identity**, not a generic theme.
2. **Genuine reviews** with photos, ideally in Arabic.
3. **An About page**, clear contact details and a WhatsApp button.
4. **A clear return policy** near the buy button. See [returns policy](/en/blog/returns-policy-uae).

### Product page
5. **Real photos and video**.
6. **The delivery date** under the price.
7. **The instalment message** under the price.
8. **A size guide** for fashion.
Details in [product page design](/en/blog/product-page-design).

### Price and shipping
9. **A final price with no surprises**: VAT-inclusive, with shipping clear from the start.
10. **Free shipping above a threshold**, with a progress bar in the cart.

### Payment
11. **Apple Pay** first on mobile. See [Apple Pay on Shopify](/en/blog/apple-pay-shopify-uae).
12. **Tabby and Tamara** for instalments. See [Tabby and Tamara on Shopify](/en/blog/tabby-tamara-shopify).

### Experience
13. **Mobile speed**. See [Shopify store speed](/en/blog/shopify-store-speed).
14. **Proper Arabic** with no RTL errors. See [Arabic Shopify store](/en/blog/arabic-shopify-store-rtl).
15. **Abandoned cart recovery** via WhatsApp and email. See [abandoned cart recovery](/en/blog/abandoned-cart-recovery).

## Test, don't guess

- Change **one thing** at a time and measure for long enough.
- Compare the same period and the same traffic source.
- Remember that conversion is affected by seasons and ads, not just design.

## FAQ

### What's a good conversion rate for a UAE store?
It varies by category and traffic source. What matters is that your rate improves over time.

### What's the quickest win?
Enabling Apple Pay and instalments, and showing delivery dates and the final price clearly.

### Does design really raise conversion?
Design that builds trust and makes mobile buying easy, yes.
