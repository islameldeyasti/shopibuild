**Short answer:** you can sell online from home in the UAE legally. The easiest start for a Dubai resident is the **e-Trader licence**, at about AED 1,370 a year. Then move from selling through **Instagram and WhatsApp messages** to **a real store** that takes online payments and organises orders and delivery, so you can grow without chaos.

## Is selling from home legal?

Yes, with a **licence**. Selling through Instagram or WhatsApp without one, however small, counts as unlicensed commercial activity. A licence also unlocks payment gateways, Tabby and Tamara, a business bank account and courier accounts.

Details in [ecommerce licence in the UAE](/en/blog/ecommerce-license-uae).

## Home businesses that work

- **Handmade products:** accessories, candles, incense burners, gift wrapping.
- **Food and sweets:** these need extra food-safety requirements depending on your emirate, so ask the relevant authority.
- **Perfume and custom blends.**
- **Fashion and abayas** of your own design.
- **Reselling products** in small quantities.

## From Instagram to a store: why switch?

Selling through DMs works at first, but as orders grow:
- Messages pile up and orders get lost.
- Bank transfers and payment confirmations are manual.
- Stock and delivery are hard to track.
- New customers don't trust easily.

**A store fixes all of this:** customers choose and pay themselves, orders are recorded, stock updates, and shipping notifications go out automatically. Instagram becomes **a marketing storefront** that sends people to the store.

## Practical setup from home

### Payments
Enable cards and Apple Pay through a gateway, and add Tabby and Tamara if your product is mid-priced or higher. See [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

### Delivery
- Local couriers that collect from your home.
- Set **fixed pickup days** to organise your time.
- As you grow, consider **fulfillment**. See [ecommerce fulfillment in the UAE](/en/blog/fulfillment-centers-uae).

### Organisation
- **A dedicated corner** for stock and packing.
- **Branded packaging**, even simple.
- **Clear reply hours** on WhatsApp.
- **Separate personal and business accounts** from day one.

## Common mistakes

1. Selling without a licence "until the business grows".
2. Mixing household money with business money.
3. Relying on an Instagram account alone (if it's suspended, everything goes).
4. Not counting the true cost: packaging, delivery and your time.

## What does it cost to start?

## FAQ

### Can I sell from home without a licence if sales are small?
No. Any commercial activity needs a licence regardless of size.

### Does the e-Trader licence allow selling through Instagram?
Yes, it's designed for selling through social media and websites. Check current conditions with the issuing authority.

### Do I need a store if I have lots of followers?
A store turns followers into buyers more easily, and protects you if something happens to your account.
