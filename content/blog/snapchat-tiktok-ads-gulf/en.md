**Short answer:** **Snapchat** and **TikTok** are among the strongest ad platforms for Gulf stores, especially for younger audiences and in Saudi Arabia. To succeed, connect your store through the **official Shopify apps** with **server-side tracking**, make **natural content in Gulf dialect** that fits the platform instead of polished ads, and start with a **test budget**, scaling the ads that drive actual purchases rather than just views.

## Why Snapchat and TikTok in the Gulf?

- **Snapchat** is very widely used in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf, with a large daily audience.
- **TikTok** has grown fast and become a key product discovery engine for younger shoppers.
- Both suit **visual products**: perfume, fashion, beauty, accessories and novel products.

## Step 1: connect the platforms to Shopify

- **Snapchat:** install the **Snapchat Ads** app in Shopify, connect your account and turn on **Conversions API**.
- **TikTok:** install the **TikTok** app in Shopify, connect your ad account and turn on **Events API**.

Make sure **purchase** events arrive with the correct value. Without accurate tracking, the platform can't learn who buys. It's the same principle as [Meta Pixel and CAPI on Shopify](/en/blog/meta-pixel-capi-shopify).

## Step 2: content that sells

Ads that look like ads get skipped. What works:

- **Short vertical video** (10–30 seconds).
- **The first 2 seconds** hook: a problem, a result or a surprise.
- **A real person** speaking in Gulf dialect.
- **The product in use**, not static images.
- **On-screen captions**, since many people watch without sound.
- **A clear call to action**: "Order now, delivered tomorrow."

Ideas that work: unboxing, before and after, trying a perfume and reacting, styling outfits, "3 reasons why…".

## Step 3: budget and testing

1. Start with **3–5 different ad concepts**.
2. Give each enough budget to gather data (several days).
3. **Pause** the losers and **scale** the winners gradually.
4. Refresh creative constantly; ads fatigue quickly on these platforms.

## Step 4: the landing page

The best ad fails if the page is weak. Make sure:
- The page is **fast on mobile**.
- Price, delivery and instalments are **clear** above the fold.
- **Apple Pay, Tabby and Tamara** are available. See [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).
- The store is **properly in Arabic**. See [Arabic Shopify store](/en/blog/arabic-shopify-store-rtl).

## Step 5: measure the right things

Don't judge ads by views or likes. Focus on:
- **Cost per purchase** (CPA).
- **Return on ad spend** (ROAS).
- **Order value** and the share of new customers.

And compare platform numbers against your actual Shopify numbers.

## Creators and influencers

Many of the best Snapchat and TikTok ads come from **creators** filming your product in their own style, and you can run their content as paid ads. Mind the regulatory requirements for influencer advertising in the UAE.

## FAQ

### Which is better, Snapchat or TikTok?
It depends on your audience and product. Snapchat is very strong in Saudi Arabia; TikTok is strong for discovery among younger shoppers. Test both.

### What budget should I start with?
Enough to test several concepts over several days. More important than the number is pausing losers and scaling winners.

### Do I need professional video production?
No. Natural phone-shot content often outperforms polished ads on these platforms.
