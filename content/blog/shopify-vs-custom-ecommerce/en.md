**Short answer:** for most stores in the UAE and the Gulf, **Shopify is faster, cheaper and more secure** than building from scratch, because hosting, security, checkout and updates are ready-made. A **custom-built store** is worth it only when you have **truly unique requirements** that can't be met within Shopify, plus **the budget and technical team** for ongoing development and maintenance.

## At a glance

| Factor | Shopify | Custom-built store |
|---|---|---|
| Upfront cost | Low to medium | High |
| Time to launch | Days to weeks | Usually months |
| Hosting | Included | On you |
| Security and card data | The platform's responsibility | Entirely on you |
| Checkout | Ready and trusted | Built and tested from scratch |
| Updates and maintenance | Handled by the platform | An ongoing team |
| Flexibility | High within the system | Almost unlimited |
| Integrations | Thousands of ready apps | Built one by one |

## The real cost of custom development

A custom store isn't a one-off cost:
- **Build:** design, development, an admin, and order and inventory systems.
- **Hosting and protection**, SSL and backups.
- **Security:** card-data protection requirements and regular reviews.
- **Maintenance:** fixes, updates and ongoing development.
- **Team dependency:** if the team changes, the knowledge leaves with them.

Compare with [Shopify store cost in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-store-cost-uae).

## "But Shopify is limited": is it?

Partly. Shopify allows:
- **A fully custom theme** in your design. See [custom theme vs template](/en/blog/custom-theme-vs-template).
- **Custom features** in the theme or through private apps.
- **External system integrations** via APIs.
- **Shopify Plus** for bigger needs in checkout, automation and multiple stores.

Many "limits" are solved by a capable Shopify developer, for far less than building a whole platform. See [Shopify development](/en/shopify-development).

## When is custom development worth it?

- Your product is itself **a platform** (a marketplace with very complex rules, or a unique booking model).
- You need **very deep integrations** with internal systems that can't connect to Shopify.
- You have **a permanent technical team** and budget for continuous development.

## The practical takeaway

Start on Shopify, launch, test the market and grow. If you genuinely hit the platform's limits years later, you'll have the sales and data to justify building something custom.
