**Short answer:** the biggest sales seasons for Gulf stores are **Ramadan and Eid**, **White Friday** in November (some platforms call it "Yellow Friday"), **11.11**, **back to school**, **Eid al-Adha** and each country's **National Day**. **Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin around 8 February 2027**, with Eid around 9 or 10 March, subject to the moon sighting. Preparation starts **4–8 weeks before** each season: stock, offers, store updates and ads.

## The season calendar

| Season | Approximate timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 11.11 | 11 November | The start of sale season |
| White Friday and Cyber Monday | Last Friday of November and after | The biggest online sale period |
| UAE National Day | 2 December | National offers and gifting |
| Dubai Shopping Festival | December to January | High shopping activity in Dubai |
| Ramadan | Starts around 8 February 2027 | Night-time shopping; gifts, perfume, fashion |
| Eid al-Fitr | Around 9 or 10 March 2027 | Peak fashion and gifting before it |
| Eid al-Adha | Per the Hijri calendar | A second fashion and gifting season |
| Back to school | August to September | Supplies and kidswear |

> Ramadan and Eid dates depend on the moon sighting; the dates above are estimates.

## Ramadan is a different kind of season

- **Shopping hours shift:** the peak is after iftar and late at night.
- **Top categories:** perfume, abayas and jalabiyas, gifts, bakhoor, sweets and hosting items.
- **Shoppers plan for Eid:** delivery before Eid is the most important promise in your store.
- **Content:** warm and family-focused, not just loud discounts.

**Tip:** show the **last order date for delivery before Eid** clearly in the header and on product pages.

## White Friday: the discount season

- Shoppers compare prices hard, so your discount must be **real** and clear.
- Launch offers **a few days early** to your list, ahead of competitors.
- Use **bundles** and **gifts with purchase** instead of deep discounts to protect your margin.

## The preparation plan (4–8 weeks before)

### Stock and shipping
- Review last season's sales and prepare stock.
- Agree **extra capacity** with your courier or fulfillment partner. See [ecommerce fulfillment in the UAE](/en/blog/fulfillment-centers-uae).

### The store
- **A dedicated season page** (such as "Ramadan gifts") in Arabic and English.
- **Banners** and offers scheduled to go live on time.
- **A speed test** before traffic spikes.
- **Payment methods:** Apple Pay, Tabby and Tamara enabled. Instalments lift basket size in peak seasons. See [Tabby and Tamara on Shopify](/en/blog/tabby-tamara-shopify).

### Ads
- Prepare **ad creative** weeks ahead.
- **Build audiences** before the season (visits, video views), then retarget them with offers.
- Ad costs rise during peak seasons, so plan your budget accordingly.

### Communication
- **WhatsApp** messages to opted-in customers before offers start. See [WhatsApp marketing](/en/blog/whatsapp-marketing-shopify).
- Reminders of the last delivery date before Eid.

## After the season

- Analyse which products, offers and ads worked.
- Follow up new customers with a second-order offer.
- Write down lessons for next season.

## FAQ

### When does Ramadan 2027 start?
It's expected around 8 February 2027; the official date depends on the moon sighting.

### When should I start preparing my store for Ramadan?
At least 6–8 weeks ahead, to cover stock, design and ads.

### Should I run deep discounts for White Friday?
Not necessarily. Bundles, gifts and free shipping protect your margin and still attract buyers.
