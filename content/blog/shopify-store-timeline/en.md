**Short answer:** designing a professional Shopify store takes **5–7 business days** with a specialist agency, as long as your products and content are ready. A full launch, including the licence, payment gateway approval and shipping, usually takes **2–6 weeks**. The biggest delays aren't design; they're **missing content and payment approvals**.

## The realistic timeline

| Stage | Typical time | Can it run in parallel? |
|---|---|---|
| Trade licence | 1 day to 2 weeks | Yes |
| Bank account | Days to several weeks | Yes |
| Content (photos, descriptions, prices) | Days to weeks | Yes |
| Store design and development | 5–7 business days | Once content is ready |
| Payment gateway, Tabby and Tamara approval | 1–3 weeks | Yes, apply early |
| Shipping setup | Days to a week | Yes |
| Testing and launch | 1–3 days | At the end |

The trick is to **run stages in parallel**: apply for your payment gateway while the store is being designed, instead of waiting for each stage to finish.

## The design stages in detail

### Day 1: brief and materials
Agree on pages, collections and integrations, and hand over your logo, colours and content.

### Days 2–4: design and build
Build the Arabic and English theme, the homepage, collection and product pages, and the cart.

### Days 4–5: products and integrations
Upload products, connect payments and shipping, and install ad pixels.

### Days 6–7: review and launch
A round of revisions, test orders with every payment method, then launch.

## What usually causes delays?

1. **Product photos aren't ready.** The single biggest cause.
2. **Product descriptions are missing**, or there's no Arabic version.
3. **Payment gateway approval runs late** because the application went in late or documents were incomplete.
4. **Endless revisions** because the direction wasn't clear from the start.
5. **Slow replies** to the designer's questions.

## How to move faster

- Prepare a **product sheet** with names in Arabic and English, prices, sizes and descriptions.
- Shoot products **on a consistent background** at the same size.
- Collect **three stores you like** as design references.
- Apply for **your gateway, Tabby and Tamara** on day one.
- Name **one person** who signs off decisions.

## Can you launch a store in a day?

Technically yes, with a free theme and a few products. But an Arabic store customers trust, connected to payments, shipping and tracking, takes days, not hours. A rushed launch usually means redoing the work a month later.
