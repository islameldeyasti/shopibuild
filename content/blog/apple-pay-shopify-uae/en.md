**Short answer:** because **Shopify Payments isn't available in the UAE**, Apple Pay on your store comes through **an external payment gateway that supports it with Shopify**. One example that lists Apple Pay support in the region is **Tap Payments**, and other gateways exist. Choose a gateway that shows Apple Pay **directly inside checkout**, activate it in your gateway account, verify your domain, then test from a real iPhone.

## Why Apple Pay matters for your store

- A large share of UAE orders come from **iPhones**.
- Shoppers pay **with a touch or a glance**, without typing card details.
- Fewer steps means **fewer abandoned carts** on mobile.
- Shoppers trust Apple Pay more than entering their card on a new site.

## Step 1: choose a gateway that supports Apple Pay on Shopify

Not every gateway supports Apple Pay on Shopify for UAE merchants. Ask the gateway:

1. **Is Apple Pay supported with Shopify** for UAE merchants?
2. **Where does the button appear?** Inside Shopify checkout, or on an external page?
3. **Is Apple domain verification needed?**
4. **What's the fee** for Apple Pay transactions? (Usually the same as cards.)

> Some merchants report problems activating Apple Pay for the UAE with certain gateways. Get confirmation in writing before you sign.

Gateway options are covered in [Shopify payment gateways in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

## Step 2: activate Apple Pay

The steps vary by gateway, but usually:
1. Turn on Apple Pay in your **gateway dashboard**.
2. **Verify your domain**: the gateway gives you a file or steps to prove domain ownership to Apple.
3. Enable the payment method in **Shopify → Settings → Payments**.

## Step 3: test it properly

- Use a **real iPhone** with a card added to Wallet.
- Test in **Safari**, where Apple Pay appears on iPhone and Mac.
- Place a full order and confirm it's marked **paid** in Shopify.
- Test in both Arabic and English.

## Common problems

| Problem | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| The Apple Pay button doesn't show | Not using Safari, no card on the device, or domain not verified |
| It shows on one version only | Domain setup incomplete for one host (with or without www) |
| The order isn't confirmed | A problem in the gateway–Shopify connection |

## Google Pay too

Don't forget **Google Pay** for Android shoppers. Many gateways support both with the same setup.

## Ordering payment methods

On mobile, put **Apple Pay first**, then cards, then Tabby and Tamara. See [Tabby and Tamara on Shopify](/en/blog/tabby-tamara-shopify).

## FAQ

### Can I enable Apple Pay on Shopify without Shopify Payments?
Yes, through an external gateway that supports Apple Pay with Shopify in the UAE.

### Why isn't Apple Pay showing on my store?
Usually because you're testing outside Safari, there's no card in Wallet, or the domain hasn't been verified with the gateway.

### Does Apple Pay cost extra?
Usually not. It's charged as a card transaction at your gateway's rate, plus Shopify's transaction fee for your plan.
