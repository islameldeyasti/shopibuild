**Short answer:** you enable **cash on delivery** on Shopify as a manual payment method under **Settings → Payments**. It lifts sales for new stores and some categories, but **refused deliveries** are its biggest cost. Reduce them by **confirming every order on WhatsApp before shipping**, adding a **small COD fee**, and **turning it off** for products or areas with high refusal rates.

## Why shoppers ask for cash on delivery

- **Trust:** the shopper doesn't know your store yet, so they'd rather pay on receipt.
- **Habit:** some shoppers have used it for years.
- **No card**, or reluctance to use one online.

Over time, as Apple Pay and instalments spread, many shoppers move to prepaid. But COD still matters for many Gulf stores.

## How to enable it on Shopify

1. Go to **Settings → Payments → Manual payment methods**.
2. Add **Cash on Delivery (COD)**.
3. Write **clear instructions** in Arabic and English: "Pay in cash or by card on delivery".
4. Agree with your courier on **how payment is collected** and when funds are paid out to you.

## The true cost of COD

| Item | Impact |
|---|---|
| Refused deliveries | You pay shipping both ways with no sale |
| Collection fees | The courier charges to collect the money |
| Delayed payouts | Funds reach you days after delivery |
| Damaged goods | A refused item can come back in worse condition |

Track your **refusal rate** regularly. If it's high, each COD order can be a loss.

## Five ways to cut refused deliveries

### 1. Confirm orders on WhatsApp
Send a message after every COD order: "Your order #… for AED … arrives in two days. Tap to confirm." Don't ship unconfirmed orders, or call the customer first.

### 2. A small COD fee
For example AED 10–15. It covers part of the cost and nudges shoppers to prepay.

### 3. Minimum or maximum order values
Allow COD above a minimum, or turn it off for very large orders.

### 4. Encourage prepayment
Offer a **small discount** or **free shipping** for paying by card or Apple Pay.

### 5. Turn it off based on data
If a product or area has a high refusal rate, disable COD for it.

## FAQ

### Does Shopify support cash on delivery?
Yes, as a manual payment method you enable in payment settings.

### Does Shopify charge a fee on cash on delivery?
Manual methods usually don't carry a Shopify transaction fee, but your courier charges a collection fee.

### How do I reduce refused deliveries?
Confirm orders on WhatsApp, add a small COD fee, encourage prepayment, and disable COD for high-risk cases.

### Should I drop cash on delivery entirely?
Not necessarily. Watch the numbers: if the extra sales cover the cost of refusals, keep it with conditions.
