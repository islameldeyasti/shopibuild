When your brand grows into sales in the millions, or sells in several GCC countries with different teams and pricing, **Shopify Plus** becomes the logical choice. ShopiBuild's **Growth Partner** package is built for this stage, starting from AED 50,000 per project.

## What we do for Plus brands

### Multiple stores
A UAE store and a Saudi store, or one store per brand, with unified inventory and operations. See [selling to Saudi Arabia from the UAE](/en/blog/sell-to-saudi-from-uae).

### Checkout
Checkout customisation through Checkout Extensibility: extra fields, messaging, in-checkout offers and market-specific logic.

### Automation
Shopify Flow workflows: customer tagging, high-value orders, inventory, and scheduled launches for seasonal offers.

### B2B and wholesale
Unlimited catalogs, customer-specific pricing and payment terms. See [B2B wholesale on Shopify](/en/blog/b2b-wholesale-shopify).

### Peak performance
Preparing the store for big launches, Ramadan and White Friday: speed, testing and a contingency plan.

### Continuous growth
A monthly team for conversion optimisation, SEO and GEO, tracking and reporting.

## Do you really need Plus?

Not every large brand needs Plus. Sometimes **Advanced** is enough and saves you money. On the first call we review your numbers and needs and tell you honestly. See [when you need Shopify Plus](/en/blog/shopify-plus-uae).

## FAQ

### How much does Shopify Plus cost?
Shopify's Plus subscription starts from about $2,300 a month. Our Growth Partner package starts from AED 50,000 per project.

### Can you move our current store to Plus?
Upgrading from a standard plan to Plus is an account change. If you're on another platform, see [migrating to Shopify](/en/shopify-migration-service).

### Does Plus solve the lack of Shopify Payments in the UAE?
No. Shopify Payments isn't available in the UAE at the time of writing, even on Plus.

### Do you offer ongoing support?
Yes. Growth Partner includes ongoing monitoring and optimisation after launch.
