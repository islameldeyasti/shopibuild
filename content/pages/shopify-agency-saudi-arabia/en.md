Saudi Arabia is **the Gulf's largest ecommerce market**. We design Shopify stores for brands in **Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam** and across the Kingdom, and for UAE brands that want to sell to Saudi Arabia. The whole process runs online.

## Shopify, Salla or Zid for the Saudi market?

Salla and Zid are strong Saudi platforms for selling locally. Shopify stands out when you want **custom design**, **selling to several countries**, or **precise ad tracking** on Snapchat and TikTok. See [Shopify vs Salla](/en/blog/shopify-vs-salla) and [Shopify vs Zid](/en/blog/shopify-vs-zid).

## What a store for the Saudi market needs

### Riyals and VAT
Prices in Saudi riyals, with **15% VAT** included or clearly stated.

### Payments
- **mada:** the most widely used local card network. Support depends on your chosen gateway, so confirm it.
- **Tamara and Tabby:** instalments are very popular, and Tamara is especially strong in Saudi Arabia.
- **Apple Pay** and cards.

### Shipping
Couriers covering the Kingdom such as SMSA, Aramex, Saudi Post and others, with clear delivery times per city.

### Marketing
**Snapchat** is very strong in Saudi Arabia, and Saudi-dialect content makes a difference. See [Snapchat and TikTok ads](/en/blog/snapchat-tiktok-ads-gulf).

### Seasons
Ramadan and both Eids, **Saudi National Day (23 September)**, **Founding Day (22 February)** and White Friday.

## A Saudi brand, or a UAE brand selling to Saudi Arabia?

- **A brand registered in Saudi Arabia:** a fully local store, with Saudi payment, shipping and tax arrangements.
- **A UAE brand selling to Saudi Arabia:** cross-border sales with customs and international shipping, or stock inside Saudi Arabia as orders grow. See [selling to Saudi Arabia from the UAE](/en/blog/sell-to-saudi-from-uae).
