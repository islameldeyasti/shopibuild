Bahrain is a small but active online market, and very close to Saudi Arabia. We design Shopify stores for brands in **Manama and across Bahrain**, in Arabic and English, connect local payments and shipping, and prepare them to expand into Saudi Arabia. Everything runs online.

## What a store for the Bahraini market needs

### Bahraini dinars
Prices in BHD, rounded sensibly for a high-value currency.

### Tax
VAT in Bahrain is **10%**. Show VAT-inclusive prices or make VAT clear before checkout, and confirm your obligations with your accountant. See [setting up VAT in Shopify](/en/blog/vat-for-online-stores-uae).

### Payments
- **Benefit:** Bahrain's main local payment network. Support depends on the gateway you choose with Shopify, so confirm it.
- **Apple Pay** and cards.
- Instalment options, depending on availability for your business.

See [Shopify payment gateways](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

### Shipping
Fast local delivery within Bahrain, and proximity to Saudi Arabia makes expanding there easier later. See [shipping to the GCC](/en/blog/shipping-to-gcc).

### Seasons
Ramadan and both Eids, **Bahrain National Day (16 December)** and White Friday.

## From Bahrain to Saudi Arabia

Many Bahraini brands sell to customers in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province. A Shopify store with separate markets shows Saudi riyals and sets Saudi-specific shipping from the same store. See [Shopify agency for Saudi Arabia](/en/shopify-agency-saudi-arabia).
