ShopiBuild is a **Shopify agency in Dubai**. We design and build Shopify stores for Dubai brands in Arabic and English, connect the payments, shipping and ads Dubai shoppers expect, and launch within **5–7 business days**.

## Why a Dubai store has its own requirements

### Same-day delivery
Dubai shoppers expect their order today or tomorrow. We connect your store to local couriers or fulfillment centres that support **same-day delivery**, and show the delivery date on the product page. See [Shopify shipping in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-shipping-uae).

### A multinational audience
A large share of Dubai shoppers buy in English, while Emirati and Gulf customers prefer Arabic. That's why every store we build is **in both languages**, to the same standard.

### Payments
Cards and **Apple Pay** are essential, and **Tabby and Tamara** are very popular. Because Shopify Payments isn't available in the UAE, we connect a suitable local gateway. See [payment gateways](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

### Licensing
The cheapest start for a Dubai resident is the **e-Trader licence**; companies choose mainland or a free zone. See [ecommerce licence in the UAE](/en/blog/ecommerce-license-uae).

### Seasons
Ramadan and both Eids, White Friday, and the **Dubai Shopping Festival** from December to January. We prepare your store for each season in advance.
