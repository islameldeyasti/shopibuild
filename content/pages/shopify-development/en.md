When your store needs more than new colours and images, you need a **Shopify developer** who understands the code: a theme built in Liquid, custom features, integrations with payment and shipping systems, and real speed on mobile. Our team in Dubai develops Shopify stores for brands in the UAE and the Gulf, in Arabic and English.

## What we build

### Custom themes
A theme built for your brand on a strong foundation, with sections you can edit in the Shopify editor without code, and full Arabic and RTL support. See [custom theme vs template](/en/blog/custom-theme-vs-template).

### Features in the theme instead of apps
Every app is a monthly fee and a source of slowdown. We build features into the theme such as:
- Bundles and "buy more, save more".
- WhatsApp buttons with pre-filled messages.
- Better filters and search.
- Size guides and product finders.
- Instalment and delivery messages on the product page.

### Integrations
- Payment gateways, Tabby, Tamara and Apple Pay.
- Couriers and fulfillment centres.
- Pixels and server-side tracking.
- External systems via Shopify's APIs when needed.

### Speed optimisation
Cleaning up leftover app code, optimising images and fonts, and simplifying the first screen. See [Shopify store speed](/en/blog/shopify-store-speed).

### Fixing and improving an existing store
Have a live store that needs work? We review the theme, apps and performance, and give you a clear, prioritised plan. See [Shopify store redesign](/en/shopify-store-redesign).

## Freelance developer or a team?

A freelance developer is great for small tasks. But when the project is a full store with integrations, tracking and post-launch support, a team gives you continuity, a fixed price and a written scope. See [how to choose a Shopify agency in Dubai](/en/blog/choose-shopify-agency-dubai).
