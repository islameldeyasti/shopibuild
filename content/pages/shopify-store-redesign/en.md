Have a live Shopify store, but visitors aren't turning into sales, the store is slow, the Arabic is broken, or the apps have piled up? You don't always have to start from scratch. We review your store, find **where shoppers drop off**, then optimise or redesign it based on what will have the biggest impact.

## Signs your store needs work

- Weak conversion despite good ad traffic.
- A slow mobile store.
- Translated Arabic with obvious RTL problems.
- Lots of overlapping app subscriptions.
- Inaccurate ad tracking (Meta numbers far from Shopify's).
- A dated design, or one that looks like competitors'.

## How we work

### 1. Audit
We review the purchase funnel in your reports, product pages, cart and checkout, speed, Arabic, apps, tracking and SEO.

### 2. Priority plan
We rank improvements by **impact and effort**: quick, high-impact wins first, such as Apple Pay, instalments and clear delivery information.

### 3. Delivery
- **Optimise** the current theme, if its foundation is sound.
- **Redesign** with a new custom theme, if the foundation holds you back. Products, customers and orders stay as they are.

### 4. Measurement
We compare before and after: conversion, speed and basket value.

## The improvements that usually matter most

- Apple Pay, Tabby and Tamara clearly offered. See [increase your conversion rate](/en/blog/increase-conversion-rate).
- A product page showing price, instalments and delivery up top. See [product page design](/en/blog/product-page-design).
- Removing unnecessary apps and building replacements into the theme. See [Shopify store speed](/en/blog/shopify-store-speed).
- Fixing Arabic and RTL. See [Arabic Shopify store design](/en/arabic-shopify-store-design).
- Accurate server-side tracking.

## FAQ

### Do I have to redesign the whole store?
Not always. Targeted improvements sometimes deliver more impact at lower cost. The audit decides.

### Will I lose data in a redesign?
No. A redesign changes the theme; products, customers and orders stay in your store.

### Does the store go offline during the work?
No. We work on an unpublished copy of the theme and publish it after testing.
