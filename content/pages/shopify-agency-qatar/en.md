Qatar has some of the highest purchasing power in the region and a multinational audience that shops in Arabic and English. We design Shopify stores for brands in **Doha and across Qatar**, and connect local payments and delivery. Everything runs online.

## What a store for the Qatari market needs

### Qatari riyals
Prices in QAR, with clear shipping rates.

### Payments
- **Local debit cards (NAPS)** and international cards. Local network support depends on the gateway you choose, so confirm it.
- **Apple Pay**.
- Instalment options, depending on availability for your business in Qatar.

See [Shopify payment gateways](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae).

### Tax
Qatar had not introduced VAT at the time of writing, though preparations are under way. Check the current position with your accountant.

### Shipping
- **A Qatari brand:** local delivery in Doha and other areas.
- **A UAE brand selling to Qatar:** international shipping with customs. See [shipping from the UAE to the GCC](/en/blog/shipping-to-gcc).

### A multilingual audience
Many residents shop in English, so a store **in both languages** to the same standard is essential. See [bilingual Shopify store](/en/blog/bilingual-shopify-store).

### Seasons
Ramadan and both Eids, **Qatar National Day (18 December)** and White Friday.
