A store that relies only on ads pays for every visitor. **Shopify SEO** brings steady free traffic from Google, and **GEO** gets your store into answers from ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Arabic search in the Gulf is far less competitive than English, and that's your opportunity.

## What we do

### Technical foundation
- Separate Arabic and English URLs with hreflang.
- SEO titles and descriptions for every collection and product.
- Structured data (Product, Organization, FAQ). See [Shopify structured data](/en/blog/shopify-structured-data).
- Mobile speed and Core Web Vitals.
- Sitemap and Google Search Console.

### Keywords
Research in Arabic and English, in your Gulf customers' own words, sorted by buying intent, with one page per keyword. See [Arabic keyword research](/en/blog/arabic-keyword-research).

### Collection and product pages
Collection copy that ranks for buying keywords, and original product descriptions instead of supplier text.

### Content
Articles that answer your customers' questions and lead them to your products. See [ecommerce blog strategy](/en/blog/ecommerce-blog-strategy).

### GEO and AI search
Direct answers, clear facts, an llms.txt file, access for AI crawlers, and presence on trusted sources. See [GEO for ecommerce](/en/blog/geo-ai-search-ecommerce).

### Local SEO
Google Business Profile, reviews and city pages. See [local SEO for a Dubai store](/en/blog/local-seo-dubai-store).

## How we measure results

- Impressions and clicks in Google Search Console.
- Organic traffic and sales.
- Rankings for target keywords.
- Referrals from AI engines.
