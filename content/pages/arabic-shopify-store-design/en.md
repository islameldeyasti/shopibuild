Most "Arabic" Shopify stores in the Gulf are English stores with a translation app on top: arrows pointing the wrong way, menus opening from the wrong side, machine-translated text. We build **Arabic Shopify stores** inside the theme, alongside an English version of the same standard, because UAE and Gulf shoppers buy in both languages.

## Translated store vs a real Arabic store

| | Store translated with an app | Arabic store by ShopiBuild |
|---|---|---|
| RTL | Gaps in sliders, menus and filters | Complete and tested on mobile |
| Font | A Latin font that "supports" Arabic | An Arabic font designed for screens |
| Copy | Machine translation | Written for Gulf shoppers |
| Speed | Extra app scripts | No translation or RTL apps |
| SEO | Depends on the app | A URL per language with hreflang |
| Subscriptions | Ongoing monthly fees | None |

## What's included

- **A bidirectional theme:** Arabic right-to-left and English left-to-right, in every element.
- **Readable, fast-loading** Arabic and English fonts.
- **A language switcher** that keeps the same page and remembers the shopper's choice.
- **Separate URLs** per language with hreflang, plus SEO titles and descriptions in each language.
- **Checkout and notifications** in Arabic and English.
- **Core copy** written in Arabic in words your customers use.

### Are all your stores in Arabic and English?
Yes. Every store we build includes both languages in the package.

### Can you add Arabic to my existing English store?
Yes. We assess your current theme: sometimes we add Arabic to it, and sometimes a new theme is faster and cheaper.

### Do I need a paid translation app?
No. We use Shopify's free tools to manage translations, and direction is built into the theme.
