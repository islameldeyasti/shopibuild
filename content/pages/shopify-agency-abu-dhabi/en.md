We design Shopify stores for brands in **Abu Dhabi, Al Ain and Al Dhafra**, in Arabic and English, and connect payments and delivery across the emirate and the rest of the UAE. The whole process runs online, from the first call to handover and training.

## What makes an Abu Dhabi store different

### A strong Arabic-speaking audience
Emirati and Arab customers make up a large share of shoppers in Abu Dhabi, so the quality of the **Arabic version** is decisive: a clear font, correct RTL, and copy written for your customers rather than translated. See [Arabic Shopify store design](/en/arabic-shopify-store-design).

### Licensing in Abu Dhabi
Abu Dhabi has its own licensing routes for commercial and online activity through its Department of Economic Development, plus free zones in the emirate. Choose the route that suits your business and confirm current conditions with the authority. See [ecommerce licence in the UAE](/en/blog/ecommerce-license-uae).

### Delivery
If your stock is in Dubai, delivery to Abu Dhabi is usually next day, and some couriers offer same day. If your stock is in Abu Dhabi, you can deliver faster within the emirate. We show shoppers the right delivery time for their area. See [Shopify shipping in the UAE](/en/blog/shopify-shipping-uae).

### Payments
Cards, Apple Pay, Tabby and Tamara through a local gateway, since Shopify Payments isn't available in the UAE.
