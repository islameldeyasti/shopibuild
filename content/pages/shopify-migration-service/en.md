Running a store on **Salla**, **Zid**, **WooCommerce** or another platform, and want to move to **Shopify** without losing your products, customers and Google rankings? We handle the whole migration: data, new design, redirects, payments and shipping, and a launch with no break in sales.

## What we migrate

| Item | Migrated |
|---|---|
| Products, variants, images and prices | Yes, fully reviewed |
| Customers | Yes (passwords are reactivated via a link) |
| Past orders | Yes, depending on the platform |
| Reviews | Usually |
| Pages and articles | Yes |
| Old URLs | 301 redirects for every important URL |

## Migration steps

1. **Audit the current store:** URLs, pages that get traffic, and apps you depend on.
2. **Move the data** to Shopify and review it in Arabic and English.
3. **Design the new store:** a custom theme, faster and clearer than before.
4. **Redirects:** every old URL redirects to its new equivalent so you keep your rankings.
5. **Payments, shipping and tracking:** fully connected and tested.
6. **Launch and switch the domain** at a quiet time, then monitor errors for two weeks.

## Why brands move to Shopify

- **Custom design** that sets them apart from stores on the same templates.
- **Selling to several countries** from one store, with currencies and languages.
- **Less maintenance** than WooCommerce, hosting and plugins.
- **More accurate ad tracking** through official integrations.

## FAQ

### Will I lose my Google rankings?
With correct 301 redirects and preserved content, any dip is temporary, and rankings usually recover within weeks.

### Does the old store go offline during the migration?
No. The old store keeps running until the new one is ready and tested, then we switch the domain.

### How long does a migration take?
A small to medium store usually takes one to two weeks, including design. Large stores need an assessment.

### How much does it cost?
It depends on product count and complexity. [Book a call](/en#lead) and we'll give you a fixed price after reviewing your store.
