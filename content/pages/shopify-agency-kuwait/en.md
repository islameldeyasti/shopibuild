Kuwait is a market with high purchasing power, and shoppers who are used to buying online and ordering from Instagram accounts. We design Shopify stores for **Kuwaiti** brands in Arabic and English, and connect the payments and shipping Kuwaiti shoppers expect. Everything runs online.

## What a store for the Kuwaiti market needs

### Kuwaiti dinars
Prices in KWD, rounded sensibly for a high-value currency (such as KWD 12.500).

### Payments
- **KNET:** Kuwait's main local card network, which shoppers expect. Support depends on the gateway you choose with Shopify, so confirm it before signing.
- **Tabby and Tamara:** both operate in Kuwait.
- **Apple Pay** and international cards.

See [Shopify payment gateways](/en/blog/shopify-payment-gateways-uae) and [Tabby and Tamara on Shopify](/en/blog/tabby-tamara-shopify).

### Tax
Kuwait had not introduced VAT at the time of writing, but this may change. Check the current position with your accountant.

### Shipping
- **A Kuwaiti brand:** local couriers within Kuwait, with fast delivery across areas.
- **A UAE brand selling to Kuwait:** international shipping with customs and longer delivery times. See [shipping from the UAE to the GCC](/en/blog/shipping-to-gcc).

### Seasons
Ramadan and both Eids, **Kuwait National Day (25 February)** and **Liberation Day (26 February)**, and White Friday.

## From Instagram to a store

Many Kuwaiti businesses sell through Instagram accounts and DMs. A store organises orders, payments and stock, and Instagram becomes a marketing storefront that sends shoppers to it.
